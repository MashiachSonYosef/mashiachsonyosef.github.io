War, Section 1

Author: Rav Kook
Book: Orot
Division: Lights from Darkness, War, Chapter 1

When there is a great war in the world, the power of Messiah awakens. The time of pruning has arrived, the pruning of the tyrants; the wicked are destroyed from the world, and the world is perfumed, and the voice of the turtledove is heard in our land. The individuals who perish without judgment, within the upheaval of the torrent of war, have in them something of the quality of the death of the righteous that atones. They rise upward in the root of life, and the very essence of their life brings a general value for good and blessing to the entire structure of the world in all its values and meanings. And afterward, when the war ends, the world is renewed with a new spirit, and the footsteps of Messiah are revealed more. And according to the measure of the greatness of the war, in its quantity and its quality, so will the expectation for the footsteps of Messiah within it grow. The world war of the present contains a terrible, great, and deep expectation, joined to all the revolutions of the times and to the sign of the revealed end of the settlement of the Land of Israel. With great understanding, with immense courage, and with deep, penetrating reflection, with longing for truth and with a clear idea, one must receive the exalted content of the light of God that is revealed in a wondrous action in the events of these wars in particular.

“Master of wars, who sows righteousness, causes salvations to sprout, creates healings, awesome in praises, Lord of wonders, who in His goodness renews every day continually the work of creation; may You shine a new light upon Zion, and may we all quickly merit His light.”
