War

Section 1

When there is a great war in the world, the power of Messiah awakens. The time of pruning has arrived, the cutting down of tyrants; the wicked vanish from the world, and the world becomes aromatic, and the voice of the turtledove is heard in our land. The individuals who perish without judgment, within the upheaval of the torrent of war, have in them something of the quality of the death of the righteous that atones. They ascend in the root of life, and the essence itself of their life brings a general value for good and blessing to the entire structure of the world in all its values and meanings. And afterward, when the war reaches completion, the world is renewed with a new spirit, and the footsteps of Messiah are revealed all the more. According to the greatness of the war, in both its quantity and its quality, so grows the expectation for the footsteps of Messiah within it. Within the present world war is an awesome, great, and deep expectation, joined to all the turnings of the times and to the indication of the revealed end in the settlement of the Land of Israel. With great knowledge, immense strength, deep and penetrating reasoning, a longing for truth, and a clear idea, one must receive the exalted content of the light of God revealed in the wondrous action of these wars especially. "Master of wars, sower of righteousness, who causes salvations to sprout, creator of healings, awesome in praises, Master of wonders, who in His goodness renews each day continually the work of Bereshit. Shine a new light upon Zion, and may we all soon merit its light."

Section 2

We look upon the early generations, those told of in the Torah, the Prophets, and the Writings, those generations that were occupied with war, and they are the great ones to whom we relate with affection and holy greatness. We understand that the soul-spark is the foundation: that state of the world, which then moved in its course, in which war was so necessary, caused those souls to appear, whose inner feeling was whole in its content. The war for their existence, the existence of the nation, the war of God, was held with inner recognition. They were mighty in spirit, and knew, in the depth of darkness, how to choose good and turn from evil: "Even when I walk in the valley of death-shadow, I will fear no evil." When we contemplate them, with all the spiritual appearance for which we so long, we long for their strength, for the life-force, hewn and crystallized, firm and solid, that dwelled within them. And from this longing, our spiritual power is strengthened and the power of our might is refined, and those strong souls return to live in us as in ancient days.
