the chain · nahum

This is the same book this site serves, with the meaning taken out. Every word is the fingerprint of its own bytes, every reading is the fingerprint of its own text, and the links under a word are what that word's card prints when a reader presses it, read off the reading page itself. Nothing here is written by this project. Strip the language away and only the joins are left, so you can walk the whole book without reading a sentence of our prose or believing any of it.

This page prints the first 6 links a first visit is shown under each word and says how many it left out. chain-v1.json.gz beside it carries them all, in both states, with every fingerprint at its full 64 digits.

how to recompute any number on this page
the function
sha256 over the UTF-8 bytes of the string, with no normalisation, no trimming and no case folding. Printed as the first 12 hex digits; all 64 for every one of them are in chain-v1.json.gz beside this page.
a word
the Hebrew as the page prints it: vowels, cantillation and all, the licensed text untouched. The pale number beside it is its consonantal key.
a reading
a pill's text exactly as the card prints it. The pale number after it is the label of the source the pill's title names. Open the reading page, press the word, hash each pill: those are the links under the word here, in the card's own order.
at rest, and lifted
the cards were read twice. At rest: as a first visit finds the page, nothing kept on the device. Lifted: with every switch that withholds a reading lifted, which is the pointing kept whatever a source points, a source's own signs printed and every source on. The file holds both; this page prints the first.
what is not here
only the word is pressed. A division or a block the card offers and does not open on is named in the file and its readings are a press away: they are not in it. At rest that is 973 buttons on 298 cards.
a mark
the scribes' marks (the end of a verse, a section break, the bar between two words) keep their place and are not words. The number is the mark as the page prints it.
a word written one way and read another
the number is the whole word as the page prints it; the links are those of the card the page opens for it, and the file names its halves.
what it proves
custody: the bytes served are the bytes received, and the line from a word to a reading goes where this site says it goes.
what it does not prove
that any dictionary is right. A fingerprint is a fact about a file, never about the truth of what the file says.
