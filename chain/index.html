the chain

These are books this site serves, with the meaning taken out. Every word is the fingerprint of its own bytes, every reading is the fingerprint of its own text, and the links under a word are what that word's card prints when a reader presses it, read off the reading page itself. Nothing in them is written by this project. Strip the language away and only the joins are left, so a stranger can walk a whole book without reading a sentence of our prose or believing any of it.

The two numbers under each book are the sha256 of the files themselves, as served. Download one, hash it, compare. If it matches, everything inside it is the copy this page stands behind — and every fingerprint inside it can be recomputed the same way, with a tool that is not ours.

how to recompute any number on this page
the function
sha256 over the UTF-8 bytes of the string, with no normalisation, no trimming and no case folding. A book's pages print the first 12 hex digits; all 64 for every one of them are in that book's chain-v1.json.gz. The two numbers on this page are sha256 over the FILES, byte for byte as served.
a word
the Hebrew as the reading page prints it: vowels, cantillation and all, the licensed text untouched.
a reading
a pill's text exactly as the word's card prints it. Open the reading page, press a word, hash each pill: those are the links under that word, credited to the source the pill's title names.
at rest, and lifted
the cards were read twice: as a first visit finds the page, and with every switch that withholds a reading lifted. Each book's file holds both.
what is not here
only the word is pressed. A division or a block the card offers and does not open on is named in the file and its readings are a press away.
what it proves
custody: the bytes served are the bytes received, and the line from a word to a reading goes where this site says it goes.
what it does not prove
that any dictionary is right. A fingerprint is a fact about a file, never about the truth of what the file says.

This is 12 books of the 40 the reading side serves — 14,354 words of 453,713. The rest are not here and the reason is size and time, not doubt: every word of a chained book is pressed twice and its card read, and a word here costs about 1,956 bytes once its links are written out, so chaining the whole shelf would be near 846 MB of files. Every book that is here was read off the same reading page by the same command. Nothing was chosen for being clean.

Read off the reading pages by tools/build-chain-book-v2.mjs: every word pressed, its card read. If a book here and the book it mirrors ever disagree — a word missing, a link moved, a count off — one of them is wrong, and both are published.
