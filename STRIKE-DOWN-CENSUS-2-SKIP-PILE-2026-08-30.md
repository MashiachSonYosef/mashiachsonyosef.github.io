## The 14 reds: real, honest, and mostly surgical

All RED_CAPTURE_MARKUP_LEAK. Spot-verified by independent stream scans —
counts match (semachot 15/15; my looser regex reads 3 vs 2 and 40 vs 38 on

Avot is 4,706 rows held for 2 tokens — and every sample shows the leaked

judgment cost. The exact per-work token lists are derivable from the
census's own paths.
