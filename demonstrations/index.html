The eight rules

Eight rules of the frame, each on one short passage, opened by the reader itself. For a year these were drawings: a page that imitated the reader's card with typed Hebrew inside it, which demonstrates a drawing. Each of these is a real zone, built by the builder every served book goes through and opened by the same reader with the same card, and branded an instrument so that no gate can mistake it for a work.

The C0 never moves. Whatever a reader selects, the ink stands exactly as the source wrote it. Selection governs what is defined, never what is displayed.

1qere and ketivC0 · one for the sitecarried

One occurrence, one C0. The complex is the word; the breakdown happens a layer down.

Where the reading differed from the letters the scribes had received, they changed nothing: the written form was kept and the read form recorded beside it. The site is one word of the book, counted once, and its ink carries both forms in the source's own order.

The passage is tanakh/nahum tanakh-nahum-kq-1, printed as moses-ledgers/kq-reseal-v1/streams/tanakh-nahum-kq-resealed-v1.tokens.csv.gz wrote it.

open the passage and press either half

2maqafC0 · one per wordcarried

Two words, two C0s, two cards. Nothing is grouped over them: the count follows the word, not the typography.

A maqaf joins words into one breath and one accent. Each word it joins is its own word of the book with its own card, and the joiner rides as ink on the word before it, which is the word that gave up its accent.

The passage is tanakh/ruth ruth-1-16, printed as moses-ledgers/maqaf-reseal-v1/streams/tanakh-ruth-maqaf-split-v1.tokens.csv.gz wrote it.

open the passage and press either word of a joined pair

3abbreviationC0 · one, oncarried

One C0 for the abbreviated form, always on. The words it stands for get no C0 at all.

An abbreviation is one token standing for several words. All of them are read, but their letters are not in the ink, so the token is the word of the book and the words it stands for are a grouping of their own. The mark between the letters is part of the key, whichever character the source wrote it with.

The passage is chasidut/maor-vashemesh maor-vashemesh-bo-bo-15, printed as the verified July body, shard cleanroom-c0-ledger-map-rebuilt-active-000000000000000003740849-000000000000000003840848.csv.gz wrote it.

open the passage and press an abbreviated word

4setumahC0 · one, offcarried

One C0, off by default, keyless. It marks structure, not a word.

A closed section mark is the scribes' bookkeeping: it says a section ends here and the next begins on the same line. It is not a word of the book and it opens no reading, so it is off until a reader turns the marks on.

The passage is tanakh/exodus exodus-20-13, printed as cleanroom-c0-ledger-map-rebuilt-active-000000000000000069794244-000000000000000069808717.csv.gz wrote it.

open the passage and press the mark itself

5petuchahC0 · one, offcarried

One C0, off by default, keyless. Its own kind, beside the closed section.

An open section mark says a section ends here and the next begins on a new line. Like the closed section it is the scribes' bookkeeping rather than a word of the book, and it is off until a reader turns the marks on.

The passage is tanakh/genesis genesis-35-22, printed as cleanroom-c0-ledger-map-rebuilt-active-000000000000000069828900-000000000000000069846706.csv.gz wrote it.

open the passage and press the mark itself

6inverted nunC0 · one per word, every one cardedcarried, mark typed

Every word inside gets its own C0 and its own card. The brackets are pressable and are never toggleable.

Two inverted letters stand around a short passage, and the tradition reads them as a statement about the passage itself rather than as bookkeeping. So the words inside are ordinary words with ordinary cards, the brackets can be pressed to say what they are, and no toggle may hide them: they speak about the text.

The passage is tanakh/numbers numbers-10-35+numbers-10-36, printed as cleanroom-c0-ledger-map-rebuilt-active-000000000000000070166543-000000000000000070180865.csv.gz wrote it. The mark itself is typed, and the page says so on the word it is typed on: the two inverted nunim are absent from every source this project holds: the probe byte-scanned all 4,646 shards of the verified body and every reseal stream and found no U+05C6. The words between them are the body's own ink; the brackets are typed so the rule can be shown at all.

open the passage and press a bracket, then a word inside

7half of the bookC0 · one, offcarried

Included, with its own C0, behind the toggle the section marks share.

The scribes counted, and wrote their counting down: the middle of a book by verses or by words, and the tallies at its end. Those notes are text about the book rather than the book, so they are carried, they are pressable, and they sit behind the same toggle as the section marks.

The passage is tanakh/haktav-vehakabalah tanakh-haktav-vehakabalah--haktav-vehakabalah-postscript, printed as cleanroom-c0-ledger-map-rebuilt-active-000000000000000149689492-000000000000000149718405.csv.gz wrote it.

open the passage and press the note

8dibbur hamatchilW · a run, not a C0carried

No C0 of its own. The words are ordinary words; what is recorded is that a run of them is set apart.

A commentary opens by quoting the words it is about, and the printers set that quotation apart. Stripping the setting apart as though it were transport loses a distinction the source drew, so it is recorded as an overlay over the run before anything is stripped: the ink stays what it was and the run says what it is.

The passage is midrash/matnot-kehunah-on-ruth-rabbah matnot-kehunah-on-ruth-rabbah-petichta-petichta-1-6, printed as the verified July body wrote it.

open the passage and press a word of the opening run

Every rule below is one of three things and says which on its face: carried whole, with its source named and hashed; carried with one mark typed into it because no source we hold writes that mark there, with the typed positions named in the record and drawn as typed on the word; or typed outright, with the reason and the wire named. No demonstration is a work: each is branded an instrument in its own file, none is listed on the door among the books, none is built by the fleet, and none is served as a work anywhere. Recorded in data/rule-demonstrations-v1.json; the passages are built into zones by tools/build-demonstrations-v1.mjs and opened by the reader itself, so what answers a press here is the card that answers on every book.
