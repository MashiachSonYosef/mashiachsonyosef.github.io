# 999 Footsteps editorial charter

## Purpose

The 999 is an editorial collection, not a reward for whatever happened to be easiest to materialize. Its center is the Hebrew textual tradition of redemption: covenantal peoplehood, prophecy, exile and ingathering, collective justice, language and derivation, the restoration of divine presence, and the Kabbalistic image of concealed radiance beneath damaged garments. Elijah, Joshua son of Nun, the prophetic fifties, Carmel and divine fire are recurring interpretive constellations, not keyword quotas.

Halakhah remains indispensable as the constitutional and practical skeleton of the tradition. It does not, however, receive unlimited slots merely because a source system divided a code into many child titles. The collection should preserve the legal canon while making room for the prophetic, mystical, linguistic, historical, poetic, and national materials that explain what the law is for.

## What consumes one X slot

1. A slot represents an intellectually distinct underlying work or a traditionally recognized independent corpus volume.
2. A commentary, translation, alternate edition, source witness, or digital segmentation does not consume another slot. It remains attached to the underlying work through its own B/C0/Y provenance.
3. A multi-volume authored work normally counts once unless its volumes are independently titled and conventionally read as distinct works.
4. Tanakh follows the twenty-four-book Jewish count. Samuel, Kings, Chronicles, and Ezra-Nehemiah each count once; the Twelve count once. Their source/index children remain available beneath those works.
5. Mishnah, Bavli, Yerushalmi, and Tosefta tractates may count independently because tractates are durable intellectual units. Parallel editions of the same tractate do not.
6. Mishneh Torah consumes one collection slot. Its fourteen-book architecture and individual halakhot remain first-class children beneath that work.
7. Collections such as Tur, Beit Yosef, and Shulchan Arukh are not multiplied merely by their four-part source layout.
8. Regrouping never deletes source material. Material removed from a numbered slot becomes an attached witness, component, or recoverable reserve option.

## Editorial tests

Every slot must answer at least one strong question:

- Does this work carry a foundational layer of Hebrew scripture, oral Torah, prayer, law, mysticism, ethics, or collective memory?
- Does it materially illuminate redemption, exile, ingathering, prophecy, peoplehood, justice, language, or historical recurrence?
- Does it preserve a distinct school, era, geography, or mode of Hebrew thought that the rest of the collection cannot substitute for?
- Does it supply unusually fertile Hebrew for the derivational and lexical project?
- If it is primarily procedural, is it a landmark work rather than a redundant digest or source-system subdivision?

## Collection posture

The revised 999 should keep foundational legal sources, but derived legal redundancy is the first place to recover capacity. Newly recovered capacity should preferentially strengthen:

- Kabbalah, Hekhalot, the Ari, Ramchal, the Gra, and later redemptive mysticism;
- prophetic and aggadic treatments of Elijah, Joshua, Ephraim, kingship, exile, return, fire, garments, radiance, and divine presence;
- Hebrew grammar, roots, lexicography, Masorah, rhetoric, and theories of language;
- Jewish peoplehood, national revival, ingathering, collective responsibility, and land;
- liturgy and piyyut as lived language of exile and redemption;
- historically significant Hebrew poetry, essays, and narratives, especially material already preserved in Project Ben-Yehuda;
- selected law and responsa where they reveal communal constitution, land, labor, family safety, justice, sovereignty, or preservation of life.

## Audit standard

The final collection must have exactly 999 numbered slots, a row-level disposition for every old slot, explicit component/attachment mappings for collapsed rows, provenance and rights evidence for every new source, and validator-backed propagation through the downstream ledgers. The 2026-07-16 lock remains recoverable evidence; it is not the editorial authority for the revision.
