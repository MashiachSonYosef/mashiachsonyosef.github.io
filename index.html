Title: Mashiach Son Yosef

Translations and interpretations with an emphasis on The Ari, The Gra and Rav Kook.

Goal: to channel / of the twin / of the first yield.
Origin 1: caol — narrow channel. Flow under constraint.
Origin 2: ben te'om — of the twin.
Jeremiah anchor: רֵאשִׁית תְּבוּאָתֹהּ — the first of His yield.
Tikkunei Zohar: Bereshit is read through Jeremiah's "the first of His yield."
Translator's Note: "First" is read as the primary reducible takeaway. Yield leaves the vessel while the vessel remains living. Like "mother" means progenitor, "first yield" means output from a living source.
