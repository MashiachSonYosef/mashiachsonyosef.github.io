Primordial Sorting

  1. Gather the source text in its cleanest form, without commentary crowding the page or later edits masquerading as original phrasing.
  2. Separate literal meaning from tonal force. A phrase may be accurate and still betray the pulse of the Hebrew.
  3. Keep a visible record of disputed choices, so interpretation stays accountable instead of disappearing into a polished sentence.
  4. Publish only what can survive rereading. Everything else remains in the workshop until the language settles.

Starting Text

Orot, War

This first page is intentionally provisional. It marks the beginning of a translation archive focused on difficult, luminous texts that resist casual paraphrase. The work begins with Rav Kook on war, where catastrophe and renewal stand too close together for sentimental language to survive.

Final translations, source alignment, and annotation layers will be added in stages. For now, the page names the project, establishes the order of work, and leaves a deliberate dark field open for what will be brought into speech.
