## 2. targum-jonathan-on-joshua, 658 vs 656 — the two units are 21:36 and 21:37

Not a numbering artifact. **Joshua 21:36–37** — the Levitical cities of
Reuben (Bezer, Jahzah, Kedemoth, Mephaath) — are among the best-known
verse-presence disagreements in the Hebrew Bible: present in some Masoretic
manuscripts and absent from others, present in the Septuagint and the
Targum tradition, and printed Hebrew editions still differ. The Masorah
itself marks the problem.

So your coverage check caught, automatically and in a live work, the first
real instance of the owner's edition-scoping ruling — and it is stronger
than a numbering divergence: it is a disagreement about whether two verses
EXIST. Our Joshua's base edition omits them; the Targum's base included
them. Both are correct witnesses to their own editions, and neither should
be corrected toward the other. What it needs is the edition named on both
sides, which is exactly what the ruling asks for.
