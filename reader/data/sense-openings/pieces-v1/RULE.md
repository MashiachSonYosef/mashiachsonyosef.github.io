# RULE — pieces that are not readings (pieces v1)

13 November 2026 AD. Corpus lane (Moses). Written before the counter. Counts and lists; nothing served is changed, and no ledger's answer is changed: every branch named here was answered by the owner, and a new answer is his.

## What is asked

The serving lane's ask 5 of 12 November: thirteen pills of the outside reader's fourteen verb cards of Genesis 1 are a fragment of a dictionary line standing as a reading, and a careful reader took them for breakage. "A look at the branches that cut these."

## The families, and what this reads for each

1. **The UBS dictionary's "literally: …; hence: = …"** (six pills). The UBS ledger's branch B01, read from the served declaration (`reader/data/ledgers-v1/ubs_sdbh_hebrew_v0_9_2/declaration.json`) and the served answers (`reader/data/ledger-branch-answers-v1.json`): its question, its options, the owner's answer and the rows it covers.
2. **The unabridged BDB's openings of a numbered sense** (four pills). Not a cut: the catalog made a route of the words that open each numbered sense of an entry (its citation ends `#route:<n>:numbered_point_<point>._lead`), and BDB opens a numbered sense with what the verb is said of as often as with a rendering. Listed: every served row of M46 whose catalog rows (by the store's own join, as row entries v1 reads it: the row's cell and route text) are all such openings, with the entry, the point and the first word; and, apart, the rows reached by both an opening and another kind of route.
3. **Strong's "(absolutely) …; (qualified) …"** (one pill). The Open Scriptures Strong's ledger's shape `semicolon.top_before_bracketed_label`, which divides by the ledger's own reading and has no branch: its count and examples, from the served declaration. And the space before a comma in the pill, which relay v168 records as the lane's own and closes at print.
4. **Strong's «set with sow(-er)»** (one pill). Not a cut: the rendering as both editions print it, read from each provider file (`HebrewStrong.xml` H2232 and the Wikisource edition's ==2232==).
5. **Classical Hebrew Words' «Bara»** (one pill). Not a cut: the record the row comes from, its part of speech and where it is found, read from `classical-hebrew-words.json`. Listed: every served row of M23 whose catalog records are all names, and how many of them stand on a verb's letters in the binyan ledger's file 1. A record is a name when its part of speech, with its full stops, square brackets and spaces taken off and in lower case, begins `prn`, `pln`, `propn` or `prpn`, or is `prm` (a person, a place, a deity, a month); a gentilic (begins `gent`: Edomite) is counted apart. (Amended after the first run, which read only a part of speech beginning `prn` and so missed `pr.n.m.` and the place names.)

## The reader's thirteen

For each of the thirteen pills, from the serving lane's `cards-genesis-1-verbs.json` and `verb-cards-cross.tsv`: its family, what made it, and the row or the declaration that says so.
