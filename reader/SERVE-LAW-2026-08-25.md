# The serve law · recorded 2026-08-25

The owner ruled it on 2026-08-25, and this file writes it down where the
tools can point to it:

**Admission is the pipeline's. If a work passes the gates it goes in; if it
fails, it is held — automatically, with the reason printed. Nobody admits a
work by hand, and works are never added one by one.**

## What the ruling means here

1. **The gates decide, not a person.** A work reaches the serve set by
   passing the gates, and only that way. The owner is not a gate. The one
   thing declared by hand is a source's licence, once — and the sealed
   artifacts already carry that finer than a source: the rights catalog
   rides **per occurrence**, with display, distribution and commercial
   axes, so the pipeline holds at the exact grain the record supports.

2. **The failure this law answers.** A build once let unlicensed material
   through. Under this law that material is impossible to admit by
   politeness or oversight: either the whole work is held (its rights
   unresolvable, its apparatus uncarried — the pair law), or the exact
   word, line or section at fault is withheld while the lawful remainder
   serves, and the page says so where the withheld thing stood.

3. **Scale is the point.** The derived ranges record names every work's
   C0 range from the atlas (nothing typed); the fleet plan says per work
   what stands between it and being built; the fleet driver
   (tools/build-fleet-v1.sh) takes every staged work through the same
   serve-and-zone chain the planned works ride. Adding a work is not an
   act — it is the state of the records.

4. **The frame is complete, or it is not a frame** (the owner's
   correction, same day). The rejection lane covers every work the
   record names, always: a work is serving, or held with its reason
   said, or awaiting its text — and all three states are states OF the
   system, visible in it, never an absence from it. There are no
   per-shelf launches; there is one frame, and a shelf "launches" by
   its works' states changing inside it. A held work is not excluded
   from the launch — the hold, said plainly, is part of what launched.

## What this law does not change

- The gates themselves: the pair law (kq-rule-v1), the licence-name law,
  the export gate, the rights axes, the oracle check against the sealed
  CLI. This law makes them the *only* judges; it softens none of them.
- The HOLD on the v4 integrity question — that is an integrity matter
  between custody and its seal, not an admission matter.
- The deploy step: a red suite deploys nothing.

## Dies when

A later ruling by the owner replaces it, or the corpus lane carries an
admission law of its own that says more than this one.
