The chain's links are what each word's card prints, read off the page, and its check opens the cards

An outside reader hashed what the cards of Obadiah 1:1 to 1:6 print and looked for each pill in the chain book: of 1,915 pills 171 were no link, and 62 of the rest named another source than the card credits. Reproduced on the served build: 1,755 of 1,915 are a link under their word and 160 are not (81 a reading of a dictionary beside the store, at least 51 the store's spelling where the card prints its own, 5 a capital), 62 credited otherwise, and 1,974 links under those words that no pill prints. The chain's page said a link was "the reading's English text as the card prints it". Its builder took the route store's pool for the word's key with the store reader's own function, and the check that guarded it (check-chain-book-v1, K3) recomputed each link with that same function and never opened a card.

The mend is the plain list's: the chain is a recording, so there is no second copy of the page's rule to drift.

- tools/card-readings-v1.mjs is the one reading of a card. A word is pressed as a reader presses it and the card the page opens is read when its readings have arrived: its rows of buttons with the one pressed, the lines it prints above its pills, and every pill with the year and the source its title says and the sources behind it. Twice, each a visit of its own: at rest, as a first visit finds the page, and with every switch that withholds a reading lifted (the pointing kept whatever a source points, signs printed, every source on). Neither is derived from the other: one reading prints in the spelling of the first row the switches keep, and that row can differ. Held in the reading itself: the card's own head must name the word that was pressed; after the walk the lines under the words must be the lines before it and no word may be ruled; and what the page fetches late is waited for by name before any card is read.
- tools/build-chain-book-v2.mjs replaces v1. It fingerprints what was read and writes each book's file, its page and the index. A file names the page and every file the page asked for, each by its sha256; one still of this tree is kept; the index is not written over one that is not; --verify reads every book again and fails on a difference.
- tools/check-chain-book-v2.mjs replaces v1 and opens the cards.
- tools/round-v1.sh reads the chain where the page is whole, after the records and the lines; the search index, which the old chain step also baked, gets a step of its own.

What a link is now: under a word, the pills its card prints when the word is pressed, in the card's order, each with the source its title names. Only the word is pressed. A division or a block the card offers and does not open on is named in the file and its readings are a press away; the file and its page say so and count them (28,758 buttons on 9,304 cards). For a word the sources divide that means the chain now holds the place's own reading, which is what the card opens on, and no longer the whole form's pool behind the other button.

What was read: 12 books, 14,354 words, 25 of them written one way and read another. At rest 435,246 links on 14,341 cards, and 13 cards print none; lifted, 734,374. 27,865 lines above the pills. 14.8 MB of files and 5.0 MB of pages, where the old chain was 21 MB. Read a second time in full, the twelve files are the same bytes.

The reader's audit, repeated: Obadiah 1:1 to 1:6 at rest, 1,915 pills, all 1,915 a link under their word, at the same place in the list, credited to the source the chain names, and no link without its pill; lifted, 3,440 of 3,440.

The check. Offline, on every word of every book: the shape and the corpus fingerprints against the zones; each file is of this tree (6,465 file fingerprints taken again) and its two states are the states it says; each file hangs together; its counts are what a walk finds; its page prints its first links in order; the index's sha256 of each file is the file's; no Hebrew and no reading is printed. In a browser, on the middle chapter of every book (2,838 words): every word pressed, its card hashed there, and it is the card the chain holds, at rest (89,878 pills) and lifted (151,245); and by a second road at rest, the pool the page itself says it offered is pill for pill what the chain holds (89,263 readings). It was made to fail three ways on a copy and put back: two links swapped past the page's window in a list printed only at rest (C1 names the pill; the second road fails too), the same in a list printed only lifted (C2), and a data file the cards were read off made one byte longer (K3, and the builder refuses to write the index, in words).

What the check caught in my own first build. It starts in the middle of a book, and the first word it pressed had no line from the suggested source where the chain had one. The page fetches the suggested source's file for a book only when the first card of a visit opens, and opens that card again when the file is here; the builder, reading from the top, had recorded each book's first word without that line. The reader now opens one card and waits for the file by name before it reads any.

Found on the way, and not changed here. Two pills of one card can print one text (12 in the twelve books; they are two links, counted). Behind it: where the corpus lane's map classes a reading as a pointer, the pill prints the target entry's first English with the pointer in brackets, and the reading's own words are not on the pill. Of 2,236 resolved readings 409 have English of their own first and 1,115 say more after the target; the target is found by its letters alone (a pointer to chómer prints chamár's "Someone who leads a donkey or donkeys"); at Hosea 4:14 English Wiktionary's "they" prints as "Used as a copula: is.". The classes and the resolution are the corpus lane's and a letter has gone to it; what a reading with English of its own should print is the owner's call.

The page changes by one comment, which named the chapter check's old version (owed from the last commit), so every work's lines were read again: the same 400,242 lines.

After it ran, the chain check was set to read with two browsers at once (3 minutes 25 seconds, where it took 6 minutes 45 inside the suite) and one key that withholds nothing was taken out of the lifted state; the chain check, the six checks that read the tools, two books read again and the three tamper tests were all run again on the result.
