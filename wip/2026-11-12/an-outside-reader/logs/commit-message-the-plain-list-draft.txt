The plain list under a chapter is the reader's own lines, read off the page, and the door refuses a list that is not

An outside reader set the plain list under Genesis 1 beside the lines the reader draws above it and found them two books. The list was built by the door from the zone's baked table, oldest witness first. Until 6 November that was also what the reader drew; that day the resting line became the reading of the place's own source and then the pointing's leader, and the list went on printing the table. The check that guards the list (check-chapter-pages-v2, P3) held it to the same table and, in a browser, asked only that three of a verse's readings be found somewhere on its lines. It held the page to its builder, not to the reader.

Proven on the live build. Genesis 1 at its own address: the list holds 434 readings and the reader draws 430 lines; the same reading stands in the same place at 205; in none of the 31 verses is the list the line throughout. Verse 1 in the list read "in + beginning · create · judges · sign · the + heavens · and + <obj.> · the + earth"; on the line, "in + beginning · he created · God · <obj.> · the + heavens · and + (a gap) · the + earth". On this build: 430 of 430, 31 of 31.

The mend: the list is a recording. A second copy of the page's rule in a builder is what drifted, so there is no second copy.

- zone.html offers its own section builder (window.__materialise), the function the whole work's export already uses, so a tool can have every section built with nothing scrolled.
- tools/resting-lines-v1.mjs is the one reading of "a line": under a section's Hebrew, every reading element that is drawn and stands in no other, in the page's order; the text with the license chip taken off; the credit what the chip itself names. Where a chip names no one source (two sources of one label carry the reading) the line is credited by that label, in the chip's own words.
- tools/bake-resting-lines-v1.mjs opens each served work as a first visit opens it and keeps one record per work in data/resting-lines/. A record names the page it was read off and every file that page asked for, each by its sha256. It reads until two readings agree, keeps a record that is still of this tree, and with --verify reads every work again and fails on a difference.
- tools/serve-tree-v1.mjs serves the tree to that browser and says which files were asked for.
- tools/build-front-door-v1.mjs prints the record line by line, each reading with its credit, and refuses a record that names another page or a file that has moved (chapter-page-rule-v3).
- tools/round-v1.sh reads the lines before it builds the door (the new step "lines").

What was read: 40 works, 453,713 words, 400,242 lines. The 39 books: 305,431 words, 302,959 lines, 2,177 of them one line over more than one word, 247 words under no line. The Tikkunei Zohar, in progress: 148,282 words, 97,283 lines. The chapter pages now print 398,550 readings on 1,224 pages.

The door refuses, in words, with zone.html one byte longer than when the lines were read, with one data file one byte longer, and with one work's record taken away; with everything back it builds. Read again in full, the 40 records come out the same.

The check. check-chapter-pages-v3 replaces v2: the list is the record on every chapter page (text and credit); the record is of this tree (every file fingerprint taken again); and in a browser, on one chapter of every book at the chapter's own address, the list is the line (1,007 verses, 13,489 lines), the line is the record, and the browser's own rendering of those lines holds the same characters. It was made to fail two ways and undone byte for byte each time: one reading changed by hand in a page's list (P3 names it), and one reading changed by hand in a kept record with the door built again from it, which the door cannot refuse (L1 and L2 name it, where the page is read again).
