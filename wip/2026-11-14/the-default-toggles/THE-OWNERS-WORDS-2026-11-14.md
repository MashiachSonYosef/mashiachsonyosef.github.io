## What the page lane builds from them

- One pill per source, saying only its name: «STEP» (where it rests), «MACULA», «UBS», «BDB», later «Jastrow». One is on at a time.
- Every default carries a second source, chosen for the reader when the default is chosen: the source that fills the most of what the first leaves, in the book being read. The note under the row says the first source's share and the second's, as percentages, and the reader can choose another second source. Whatever both leave keeps today's line.
- Measured over the 39 books (`coverage.py`, `coverage-by-book.tsv`): STEP 99.9%, then MACULA or UBS fill the 0.1%; MACULA 97.1%, then STEP fills the rest; UBS 84.1%, then STEP fills the rest; BDB 36.4%, then STEP fills 63.5% (0.1% left).
