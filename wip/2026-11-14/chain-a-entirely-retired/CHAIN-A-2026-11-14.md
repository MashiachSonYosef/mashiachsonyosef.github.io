# Chain A, entirely retired? — 14 November 2026 AD

Elijah, page lane. The owner asked: "have we entirely retired chain A". Chain A is the comma rule: the cut the page and the store's reader guessed for a source with no ledger (senses at ";", readings at the commas outside parentheses, `tools/sense-split-v1.mjs`). It retired on 7 November 2026 (main `1d2470eb35`, zone-gloss-rule-v7; its last mentions `29d50995b7`). This is what was looked at on the live build (main `2646018ea9`, gh-pages the same tree but its README), and what was mended.

## What was found

- **No code cuts.** The store's reader (`tools/gloss-store-v1.mjs`, `readingsOf`) and the page (`zone.html`, `readingsOfRow`) are the same rule: a row of a source under a ledger divides only at the marks its ledger wrote (row[7]) or prints the part its ledger names (row[8]); a row of a source with no ledger is one reading, whole. No tool, page script or build step cuts a reading at a comma or a semicolon.
- **Every served source is ledgered but one.** 128 store sources carry a ledger; the 18 catalog entries without one (Wikimedia English introductions) serve no row. The span store's 3,873 rows and Jastrow's 115,918 are all of ledgered sources. The only rows of a source with no ledger are Uhlemann 1837's (MSAM2), 1,850 in the Samaritan overlay, and they print whole, as written, until its ledger lands: that is what replaced the rule, not the rule.
- **Nothing served was cut by it.** Every zone, sidecar, line, chain book and index has been rebuilt from these readers since 7 November, the last on 13 November. Of the 106,996 readings the corpus lane shipped in its witnessed orders (`data/witnessed-orders-v2.7`), 254 have no served row behind them and are refused; none is a piece the comma rule would have cut (`logs/unaccounted-witnessed.txt`, `tools/unaccounted-witnessed.mjs`). They are rows struck or retexted since («48 marg)», a list whose parenthesis a strike broke).
- **Its words.** Three checks still described the old cut in comments as the way a row divides, and `check-ledger-divisions-v1` named the source with no ledger "on the generic rule" in its report.

## What was mended (edit 87; main `044a49ddfc`, gh-pages `10648ea23f`, deployment run 1364, live check run 62)

The three comments say now what the readers do, and the report's label reads «whole until a ledger lands: MSAM2 (overlay samaritan, 1,850 rows)». No line that reads or judges anything moved. The four checks pass against the tree (`logs/check-*.txt`); lint finds nothing new; nothing is unlanded.

The words «comma rule», «generic rule» and «sense-split» stand now only where they say the rule retired, in the records quoting the owner's word of 7 November ("so we can get away from chain A"), and in `check-ledger-divisions-v1`'s D5, which proves the cut is not made.

Not chain A, though the names are alike: the chain books (`/chain/`, the recording of every card of the twelve minor prophets) and the maqaf chain (words the scribes join with a stroke).
