# The card makes no claim: the owner and the outside auditor, 14 November 2026

The owner shared a screenshot of their exchange with the independent auditor (a separate agent reading the live site). Transcribed:

- **Auditor:** A license settles whether you may carry and show a record, not what kind of statement the record makes. The dictionary that gives "town" for Jerusalem is licensed, honest, and belongs on the shelf. The only thing to debate is the card letting a statement about the thing pass as a statement about the word. "Say only what a record says" includes saying which kind of thing it said.
- **Owner:** «the gra would argue it stands then though»
- **Auditor:** He would say it stands, and then say which level it stands on. That is all I am asking of the card.
- **Owner:** «the point is it defends against making a claim»
- **Auditor:** That moves me. Sorting pills into "meaning" and "not a meaning" would be the site making a claim, and making no claims is the whole design. My own labels today (the ~48%) were my claims, not the records'. Two things I would still hold:
  1. Where a source marks its own kind, carrying the mark is still the source talking. The card's newest lines already do this: "given as a name by its source", "of another entry", "of another stem than qal". None of those is the site's claim.
  2. A flat row is not perfectly silent. Putting "town" beside "Jerusalem" under one heading says they are the same kind of thing, and no record said that.
  So the defense holds wherever the source said nothing about its kind. Where it did speak, repeating it claims nothing.

## What the page lane takes from it

- No accuracy figure, ours or the auditor's, decides what the card shows. A judge's grade of a pill is the judge's claim. The strict re-grade running now (672 pills) is information for the owner, nothing more.
- A row is withheld or set apart only on what its own source says of it (its pointing, its number, its stem, its name mark). Silence is silence. A rule that withholds a row because its source said nothing (e.g. "bare letters and no number") would be the site's claim, and is out.
- The open work is the auditor's point 2: where a source marks a row's kind (a name; a definition of a thing, not a rendering; a form of another word; part of a longer phrase; a pointer "see …"), the card says so in the source's terms, under that kind, instead of one flat row.
