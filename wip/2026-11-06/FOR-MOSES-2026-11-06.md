## 5. The pointing grade, version 2 (for your next lattice build)

The page's pointing grade (`tools/pointing-grade-v1.mjs`, rule `pointing-grade-rule-v2-…`) now departs from your `vowelForm` in one place: it drops the dagesh of a word-initial bet, gimel, dalet, kaf, pe or tav before comparing. That dagesh is lene; it comes and goes with the word before, so it says where the word stands, not which form it is. Under the old grade a source that cites the form with the dagesh graded as "pointed otherwise" wherever the text drops it: 72,092 store rows on the 39 books, and Ruth 1:7's line went bare under "only this pointing". A dagesh inside the word still decides, because it can be the piel's doubling.

Your lattice v12 grades its cards with the old lines. The page re-projected all 39 sidecars from your v12 files under v2, which also replaced leaders that still named the withdrawn Bolls BDB. When you next build the lattice, please adopt the same line so your grades and the page's agree:

```

```
