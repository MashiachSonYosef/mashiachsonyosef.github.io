## The four the owner named for this week

1. **The comma ledger and the rest of the marks.** Parentheses are already in it: `parentheses` and `brackets` are two of the eleven named cases every declaration must cover (ask v1.1). No separate parenthesis ledger is needed.
2. **The binyan error and the vav error are one fault.** The card asks every source about the bare letters, then sorts the readings oldest first. A root's whole dictionary entry, every stem and every homograph, competes with the reading of the form that stands on the page.
   - Genesis 1:1 בָּרָא, pointing switch on "keep" (the default): 120 pills. First "he created" (TAHOT at this place), second "cut down" (Strong's KJV list, 1890, which covers every stem of the root), then "select", "feed", and Aramaic and modern rows ("son", "forest", "prairie", "a scholar", "a diver", "a maniac").
   - The switch's "only this pointing" position is broken. On Genesis 1:1 it withholds 37 of 136 records, TAHOT's "he created" among them, and the line turns to "cut down". The row grade itself matches TAHOT's headword (the page's `vowelForm` of בָּרָ֣א equals TAHOT's), so the fault lies after the grade: the at-this-place order or the piece-store overlay row.
   - The letters ו hold 191 rows. MACULA's suffix וֹ ("him", "its", "it", "his", "himself", "he") holds ranks 1 and 3 to 7; the conjunction "and" is rank 2. The key also holds BDB's letter name and three Wikisource Strong's entries misfiled under ו (the entries for "write", "not" and "fall down"). At 2 Kings 2:1:10 the line reads "and + Elisha", which is right; the vav block's card opens on "him".
   - Every store row carries its source's own pointed headwords at [6]: וְ for the conjunction, וֹ for the suffix, בָּרָ֣א for the qal and בָרֵ֥א for the piel. So the card can ask this form first without any new data. The piel "cut down" inside a dictionary's one entry for the root (BDB's "Pi . cut down cut out") needs the dictionary's own stem labels, which is where the binyan ledger meets the mark declarations.

## The other screenshots

- **Genesis 1:1:4 את.** The Strong's sense "against, among, before, by, for, from, in(-to), (out) of, with. Often with another prepositional prefix" prints whole beside its pieces, because one piece runs over four words (sense-split-rule-v3, rule 5). Under the Strong's declarations the list ends at "with." and the note after it is not a reading.
- **2 Kings 1:1:5 מות.** "e.g. ox" is a piece of M46 (unabridged BDB) "of animals killing men, e.g. ox". "with power over humankind" is a piece of M18 (UBS) prose "with power over humankind, without remembrance, without praising God". Both are pills, not the default: the line reads "[the] death of".
- **Haggai 1:4:1 ה.** "(6997a=1886a+6997)" and "X" are MACULA (M3) rows on the letter ה (headword הַ). "he (n, wikt, en 2)" is ConceptNet (M1), with a provenance tag left in the route text. "my", "one", "its own" and "one's" are MACULA rows for other ה forms, the vav fault again. The head's "¿ + [does] a time" is TAHOT's own question sign.
- **Haggai's blanks (2:9:5, 2:9:6, 2:9:13).** TAHOT writes "<the>" where English drops an article. With source signs off (the owner, 3 October: "preselect it off for now"), a piece that is only a sign is drawn as a gap. MACULA writes "the" on the same pieces.
- **Nahum 1:12:9 נָגוֹזּוּ.** The piece store has no place for it and the route store nothing under נגוזו. TAHOT and MACULA spell the word without the vav, נָגֹזּוּ, and give "they will be cut down" and "cut off" under נגזו. Across the Tanakh: 1,048 of 304,369 words have no piece-store place, almost all of them spelling (Moses's v106; this note first said 3,723, counted with each verse's marks numbered as words); 235 have no route on their letters at all; 136 of those have one under a spelling one vav or yod shorter.
- **Nahum 2:6:4, the ketiv/qere that is not selectable.** The read form's pieces 2 and 3 carry no letter offsets (null), so the card falls back to "parts of this word" and offers no block pills. The nulls are in the corpus lane's piece store v2, as received: 475 of the 578 places where a read form divides into pieces have a piece with no offsets (the first is Esther 3:4:2). Each piece's record carries its own Hebrew ("הֲלִֽיכָתָ֑" for "march"), so the pieces can be aligned to the read form's letters in order: by the page as a fallback, or by Moses in the next piece store.
- **Nahum 2:9:8–9 עמדו עמדו.** Both sources read "stop" at each place. A reading pressed on one card is remembered for its spelling, so both cards change together.
- **Lamentations 1:2, "her + with".** The one hop (the owner, 14 October) moves a piece whose English is my, your, his, her, its, our or their in front of the piece before it. English "her" is also the object pronoun, so "with her" (1:2:15), "of her" (1:2:17), "[belongs] to her" (1:2:8) and "they have overtaken her" (1:3:14) hop too. The fix is to hop only a possessive, whose host is a noun, read from the source's own grammar. Removing the hop would break "her cheek" and "her lovers". Only "her" is ambiguous (the other six words are possessive only). Measured on the 39 books: in 170 places "her" is the core of a word whose preposition is a prefix (בָּהּ "with + her"), which the place pieces' own role says (core, not suffix); the rest are suffixes, and among them a preposition written as its own word (עָלֶיהָ) and a finite verb ("he has taken + her", 117 by their English alone) must not hop either, while a participle ("her lovers", "her pursuers") and an infinitive should. The lattice pieces carry the host's class (noun, verb, prep), not whether a verb is a participle; the binyan ledger's file 1 could carry the verb's form beside its stem. Waits until after the big four.
- **Lamentations 1:3:15–16 בין המצרים.** The run opens on the joined form, one pill: "the Three Weeks". Pressing the split block puts "between + the + narrow places" in the card's head, but the line cell keeps no English. Reproduced. The cause: the words' division of a run opens the first word's card (the nesting of 16 October) and returns before it records the reader's division, so the run's line goes on reading the dictionary's whole entry. Recording the division on that press (as every other division press does) draws the words' own lines under the run.
- **Lamentations 1:5 לפני־צר.** Indent or arrow for the layers is a design question; an indent per layer is the lighter of the two.
- **Elisha.** No source on record says "(my) God has saved". BDB says "God is salvation". Nothing was dropped.

## R2 from a fresh container

The environment holds the R2 keys as lowercase variables (`access_key_id`, `secret_access_key`, `endpoint`, `R2_BUCKET`). The uppercase `AWS_*` variables are placeholders. `tools/r2-read-v1.mjs` beside this file is a read-only client that uses the lowercase names and never prints them; run it with `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1`.
