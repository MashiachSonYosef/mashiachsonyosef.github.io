## What still goes to the owner

- Anything said in the owner's own name to someone outside: a note to STEP or to another source's maintainers.
- What only the owner can reach: accounts, dashboards, bucket settings, payments.
- Downloads or hosts the environment refuses. Name them; do not route around a refusal.
- Deleting another lane's records. The corpus lane runs deletions.

Everything else is decided here and reported after.
