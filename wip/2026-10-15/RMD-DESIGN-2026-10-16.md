# RMD on the page: the design and what the skeptics found (16 October 2026)

The owner: "hopefully you get how its all formulaically rule based. you dont need a single english definition to have the compspans. i want to see us move away from this default is outside the system mentality ... each piece in the + sign is RMD (r pill, M license, D definition)".

## The rule, as designed

A compspan is the cut, not the English: the ledger's row for the form, else the source's declared pieces at the place read by their letters alone; a maqaf chain is the same rule one level up. The cut exists with or without English; the card offers every complete cover of it; the blob splits on the letters. Everything between "+" signs is a row of the store (R = slot 1, D = slot 2, M = slot 3 through the served index). Nothing is painted that no row stands behind.

## What breaks it today (zone.html)

- The line under a divided word is TAHOT's piece text joined from the zone's pg (placeLine / lineUnder), no row behind it.
- The card manufactures a row for the place and for the set ([0, text, text, mId, null, null, null] into PLACE_ROWS / SET_KEY); its D is its own text.
- The set pill stands only when the English is present, and blocks every piece pill; "here the source reads" is the same text again.
- A block left alone falls to the baked table's first word for the letters ("describe" for the vav, "beat" for the he).

## What the three skeptics refuted in the first design

- Baking per-piece rows from pg is the page's synthesized row moved to disk: Elijah would mint four of the seven slots. Only Moses's piece store can supply piece rows honestly.
- Fanning TAHOT's set into per-piece pills contradicts the owner's standing rulings of 3 October ("theyre giving us 3 definitions in 1 license. and we only apply it when the card is all 3"; "the key is to not fan out those R pills as if theyre solo ... the license is 1 whole still").
- An order ranks the form's records, never the letters': under "oldest first" a per-piece first pill reads "describe + (she) ass + they".
- 394 words carry a ledger row but a one-piece source gloss (Malachi 1:12, Genesis 18:25); 1,344 words carry the same piece key twice (Genesis 2:25): cells must be keyed by position, not letters.
- "I Kings" is the book title's tokens (work_he_tokens), laid outside any section: the span store needs title and chapter places as well as verse places.
- The BSB's source record must not be typed by Elijah; its cells carry commas and would be split into pills by the pool's grouping.

## The honest step for the page, with what the store holds today

1. Delete the page-made rows (PLACE_ROWS, the SET_KEY row). The set pill is the store's real whole-key row for the form (TAHOT's "and/ I gave/ them", M4), fetched from its shard: R from the row, M from the index, D the row's text with the piece lit.
2. The unruled line under a divided word is that row drawn as its pieces, one chip; "here the source reads" goes, being the same row. Where the store has no row for the form, the cut stands and its slots stand empty ("even if a ton are empty"); the baked table's first word for a piece's letters is never painted.
3. A block pressed alone shows the dictionaries' rows for its letters, as now; a block ruled to one is that pill on the line, the rest of the line still the form's record.
4. Cells keyed by position within the cut; the ledger row and the one-piece source gloss reconciled by letting the record stand on the whole form where its pieces do not match the row.
5. Moses's piece store, when it lands, becomes the per-piece default under "at this place"; the span store lays the underlines, verse, title and chapter places alike.
