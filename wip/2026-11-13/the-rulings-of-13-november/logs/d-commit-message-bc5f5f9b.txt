The words that open a numbered sense print as a note on the card, not as a pill

The catalog made a route of the words that open each numbered sense of an entry of the unabridged BDB, and BDB opens a sense with what the word is said of as often as with a rendering. So «object heaven and earth», «the individual man» and «of transformations: a clean heart» stood as readings of "he created" (Genesis 1:1), and an outside reader took all four such pills on the verb cards of Genesis 1 for broken pieces of a line (12 November). The corpus lane listed the served rows that are only such an opening (relay v174: 2,008 rows of the unabridged BDB, each with its entry and its point), and the owner ruled how they print (ruling 4 of relay v176, «1-5. agree go ahead»): as a note beside the sense's readings, not as a pill.

What is in the tree.

- data/sense-openings/ keeps the lane's list, rule and receipt byte for byte, named in its pin by size and sha256. tools/apply-sense-openings-v1.mjs marks each listed row in its facts (opens: the entry and the point, «BDB1300 1.»), reading the ruling from the lane's own pin of it (data/rulings/) and refusing one that says anything but "as a note beside the sense's readings, not as a pill". Every one of the 2,008 lines finds exactly one row. No text of the store is touched: its version moves from 43acd0b3d9a3 to 4d1357b26ca5, and of its 652,954 rows 650,946 are the same in every slot; the 2,008 marked rows differ in their facts alone.
- The store's reader gives a marked row no reading (tools/gloss-store-v1.mjs, senseOpened), so no line, no pill, no link and no index entry stands on it. The page carries the same function under the same name.
- The card prints the marked rows its switches keep as its notes, in one small line under the record of the reading held: the source named once, each note with its point, in the dictionary's blue («BDB unabr. opens its numbered senses with: 1. object heaven and earth · 2. the individual man · …»). It stands in the record's body, which scrolls and which no fit of the card hides. A card with no reading to hold prints its notes in the readings' band. A marked row the switches withhold is no note: the opening of another entry's sense is not said of this word.
- The witnessed columns refuse a shipped reading whose row is a note, by name («prints as a note»), as they refuse one whose row prints no pill.
- tools/page-reading-v1.mjs: the page's reading functions, read out of zone.html and run, in one place. The search index's bake and the checks of the ledger divisions and of the made spaces each lifted them for themselves, naming what readingsOfRow leans on one by one, and each list was a name short when the page's function gained its question. They read them from here now.
- The page reads a row's facts (factsOf) beside the other functions that read a row, above every gate that asks them, and tools/check-entry-gate-v1.mjs lifts it by its own text.
- PIPELINE.md's opening note says it.
