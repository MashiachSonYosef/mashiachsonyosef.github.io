Three more dictionaries name their stems, and a row beside the store is asked its stem too

Under "only this pointing" the card withholds a reading its source files under another stem of the verb than the one that stands in the verse. Until now only BDB's rows carried a stem, with the readings TAHOT and MACULA give at their own places. The rows of the UBS dictionary, of the SDBH and of Jastrow carried none, so the SDBH's «to carve» and «to fatten oneself» stood on the card of the qal "he created" (an outside reader's finding on the verb cards of Genesis 1, 12 November).

The corpus lane read the stems of those three out of the sources themselves (relay v173, 13 November 2026 AD): 31,487 lines, one for each of their rows on a verb's letters. The UBS dictionary lists the stems of each meaning. Jastrow names a stem over each block of senses; the senses a verb's entry gives before any name are its base stem by his order, though he prints no name there. The SDBH names none, and it is the same work as the UBS dictionary. The lane left those two as branches for the owner, who set both (ruling 2 of relay v176, «1-5. agree go ahead»): an SDBH row takes the stems of the UBS edition's meanings of the same entry that give the same gloss, and a Jastrow reading that stands before the first heading takes its entry's base stem. Under those settings 19,007 rows gain stems (the UBS dictionary 6,004, the SDBH 3,117, Jastrow 9,886), 6,357 of them by a branch.

What is in the tree.

- The file of lines is 7 MB and stays on the lane's store. data/stems-extension/ names it by its size and sha256 and keeps the lane's rule and receipt byte for byte. tools/stems-extension-v1.mjs reads the file only as those bytes, and reads the owner's settings from the lane's own pin of the ruling (data/rulings/): a branch set to its option gives a line that option's verdict, a branch set to NONE leaves the line as written, and any other setting is refused in words.
- The round's projection takes the file on its command line (tools/round-v1.sh --stems-extension, required with --lattice) and bakes each row's stems into every book's sidecar beside the stems it baked already, under the same id. A row that both files name is refused. Each sidecar says which file, which ruling and which settings its stems were read under, and how many rows it gave stems.
- The page asks the rows beside the store their stem. Jastrow's rows are served beside the route store and joined a card after the stem had been asked, so the store's rows were asked and they were not. They are asked where they join, after their own pointing.
- tools/rows-beside-the-store-v1.mjs says in one place which rows beside the store a card takes and what it asks them, so that a check counts them as the page does. tools/check-pointing-gate-v1.mjs counts them with the store's. PIPELINE.md's opening note names the stems, their extension and that module.

What a reader sees. On "he created" (Genesis 1:1) thirteen readings are gone: the SDBH's «to clear (by cutting)», «to carve» and «to fatten oneself», Jastrow's «to be created», «recover», «to be strong», «stout», «to get well», «to come outside» and «bore», and three of the UBS dictionary's causative senses. The card holds 58 pills where it held 71, and says «25 of another stem than qal, withheld» where it said 9. On the outside reader's fourteen verb cards of Genesis 1, set beside the reader's own call on each pill: of the 12 pills the reader called the same entry in another stem that one of these three dictionaries carries, 11 are gone. The twelfth, «to cut down», stands because Wiktionary gives the same words and names no stem. Two pills the reader called another word and two it was unsure of are gone too. Of the 132 pills the reader called right, none is gone. Across the fourteen cards 18 pills are gone and none has come.

Measured against the live build, the round run whole (40 works):

- 73,630 word places of the thirty-nine books are asked their stem. At 7,141 of them the extension withholds a row that the earlier gates leave: counted place by place, 56,947 rows of the UBS dictionary, 10,552 of the SDBH and 38,652 of Jastrow. The commonest are a hiphil reading at a qal place (41,497 rows), a piel at a qal (14,903) and a niphal at a qal (14,155).
- Lines under the words: 1 of 400,242 reads otherwise. The last word of 2 Kings 18:17 is the «fuller» of the Fuller's Field. The lattice gives that place no reading of its own, so its line is the first reading the gates leave, and it read «fuller»: a reading Strong's carries, which led because the SDBH's row for the same word stands early in the catalog. The UBS edition files «fuller» under the piel participle (the fullers of Malachi 3:2) and «Fuller's Field» under the qal. The word here is the qal participle, so the SDBH's «fuller» is withheld, the reading stands on Strong's rows alone, and «trample», which opens Strong's entry, comes before it. «fuller» and «Fuller's Field» are still on the card.
- The leader under "only this pointing", which is the line of a reader who chooses "oldest first": another reading at 23 of the 302,300 places that have one on both builds, the place above among them. None gains or loses its leader. Nine are the piel of one verb ("uncover"), where «open» reads «uncover».
- The twelve chain books, which press every word and read its card (14,354 words). At rest 13,988 cards hold the links they held and 366 lose some and gain none: 5,263 links are gone, 414,124 links to 408,861. With every switch lifted every card holds exactly the links it held (728,474).
- The search index: every count the same.
- Weight: the zones with their sidecars are 64.2 MB where they were 58.7 MB; Genesis's sidecar is 1.79 MB where it was 1.58 MB. A sidecar names each row whose stems it holds by the row's source, its rank and its whole route text, and the UBS dictionary's routes are long. Source and rank alone do not tell rows apart (144,587 rows of the store share both with another row of their key), so the text cannot simply be dropped; a short fingerprint of it would bring the stems of Genesis from 405 KB to about 100 KB, less than they weighed before this ship. That is a cleanup of its own and is not in this one.

Held by a new check. tools/check-stems-extension-v1.mjs, eight laws: the pin, the rule, the receipt and the one pinned ruling (B1); the file of lines as pinned, every line and branch well formed and ruled, and counted as the lane's receipt and the ruling count them (B2); every line finds exactly one row, 15,139 of the store and 16,348 beside it (B3); every book's sidecar names the extension and the ruling and holds exactly each line's stems (B4: 39 books, 350,636 rows given stems book by book); and four laws pressed on cards: a row beside the store whose own source names another stem is withheld and counted (Amos 1:1, 4 rows), a row of the store that has its stem by the UBS edition's branch (Amos 2:7, 25 rows), a row beside the store that has its stem by the base stem's branch (Daniel 2:19, 24 rows), each brought back by "keep", and a row beside the store of the word's own stem stands on its pill (Amos 3:6, «do»).

The check made to fail on purpose, nine ways, each file put back byte for byte and the check green again at the end: the extension's receipt as other bytes (B1, B2, B4 red); the pinned rulings as other bytes (B1, B2, B4, and the card laws find no word to press); in one book's sidecar, a row given another stem than its line, a row's stems dropped, a row no line names given stems (B4 and the card laws each time); the sidecar's record saying a branch rests at NONE (B4); its count one row high (B4 and the card laws); the page not asking the rows beside the store their stem (B5, B7, B8); and the file of lines handed to the check as other bytes (B2). The tree's changed and new files were fingerprinted before and after the nine and are the same.

Lint. The five changed tools carry no finding their copies on the live build do not, and the page carries the 175 it carried.

Suite and gate. 149 of 150 checks pass before the push; the one red is the check that the tree is pushed, which this commit answers. The same-product gate against the live build: 9,383 sampled lines the same on 18 pages; 4 of 54 first cards differ, the second word of Job and the third of Ruth, each read on two chapter pages. Every differing pill is accounted for with the extension's own stems: on "it was" (Job 1:1) four of Jastrow's piel readings are gone («to change one's legal status», «to dispose of», «to produce», «make»), and on "to judge" (Ruth 1:1) two of the UBS dictionary's niphal readings; none comes, and no card opens on another reading.

What it does not reach. Strong's rows name no stem and stand, and so do the rows of every source that names none: a reading such a source shares with a row of another stem stays on the card («to cut down» above). The lane measured borrowing a stem by the words alone and advises against it. Eleven of Jastrow's stem names are no member of a family in the family rule and no place of the thirty-nine books carries them (ittaphal, ithpolel, ithpalpal, nithpalpel, palpel, paeli, ithpaeli, nithpoel, nittaphal, pirel, pali: 61 rows). A row that holds only such a name is another stem's wherever it is asked, which is so while no place is of those stems; the families are the lane's to map, and the lane is told. Jastrow's three other partitions have no row on a verb's letters. A word the lattice gives no stem is not asked.
