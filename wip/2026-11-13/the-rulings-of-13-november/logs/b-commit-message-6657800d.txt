A pointer names its entry, a reading with English of its own prints its own words, and a pressed pointer no longer stands bare on the line

Some readings of the store are not English at all but a pointer to another entry in the source's own words: «Masculine singular imperative of הָלַךְ (halách).» The card prints the English of the entry pointed to, with the pointer beside it. Until now the entry was found by the pointer's bare letters and the first row under them was taken, so «Masculine singular imperative of הָלַךְ» printed «traveler, nomad, wayfarer», and a form of the preposition "to" printed «god (a deity or supreme being ...)».

The corpus lane reissued its map of these readings (relay v175, 13 November 2026 AD: pointer-entries-v1, zone-pills-v1.3). It reads the target from the pointer's own bytes, its points, its romanization, a gloss it quotes, and names the entry. The owner ruled on its two open calls the same day: a reading that has English of its own beside its pointer prints its own words whole (ruling 5, «1-5. agree go ahead»), and a pointer that cannot tell its entries apart takes the source's own first entry (ruling 7, «1-3. ok agree»).

What is in the tree. The lane's map, its rule and its receipt are kept byte for byte in data/pointer-entries/, and the owner's rulings as the lane pinned them, with the owner's own words, in data/rulings/. Each folder has a pin naming every file by its size and sha256. Two small modules read them: tools/pinned-v1.mjs (a file another lane wrote is read only as the bytes its pin names) and tools/rulings-v1.mjs (a tool that applies a ruling reads it from the pin and cites it in its record; it does not carry the ruling in its own code). tools/bake-form-of-pills-v1.mjs is rewritten on them: it needs no argument, refuses a map or a ruling that is not the pinned bytes, and its record names the two rulings it applied.
