The paseq the edition writes inside a read form prints there

At 16 places written one way and read another, Miqra according to the Masorah writes a paseq inside the read form: after its only read word, before the bracket closes (11 places), or between its two read words (5). The corpus lane's restore of 5 September made every scribal mark its own position, as the owner's rule 11 has it, and in doing so stood each of these strokes after the whole place; lattice v12 serves that order, and so did every zone built from it. At Job 38:1 the edition reads (מנהסערה) [מִ֥ן ׀ הַסְּעָרָ֗ה], and the reader, with the marks shown, printed (מנהסערה) [מִ֥ן הַסְּעָרָ֗ה] ׀. The lane named the defect in relay v167 and mended it in relay v179 with a list of the 16 places, each keyed to the lattice's positions and to the word place, with the place as the edition prints it. Its second count found the same 16 from the streams alone, and the Leningrad Codex sets the stroke after the same read word at all 16.

What is in the tree.

- data/paseq-place/ keeps the lane's list, rule and receipt byte for byte, named in its pin by size and sha256.
- tools/apply-paseq-place-v1.mjs writes two facts on the 12 zones the list names, and nothing else: on the place, the stroke's glyph, copied from the mark's own position, and the read word it follows; on the mark, that it prints inside the read form of the place before it. No word, position, count or key moves. It first proves each line against its zone, and any difference is a refusal that writes nothing: the place found by its verse and word number is written one way and read another, its surface is the lattice's site, its read words are the line's count, and the word after it is a paseq; where a maqaf joins the place to the word before, the two together are the lattice's row; and the place drawn as the page draws it, with the stroke after its read word, is the line's own print, character for character, at all 16. The write is typed on each zone it changed (single-pass-exemption-v1) and expires when the zones are built from the lane's next lattice, which stands each stroke in its place itself. The round's three zone tools carry both facts, the record and the typing through, shown on Job: the witnessed bake, the re-gloss and the lattice projection.
- The page: one rule says where the stroke stands (strokeIn), after the read word the zone names, inside the bracket where that word's own region closes it. On the line an empty span stands there, and the stylesheet draws the zone's glyph in it when the marks are shown, dressed as every mark is. The span holds no text, so the place's text is its carrier for everything that reads the line: the card's head, a copy, every check that reads a drawn word. The mark keeps its block, its position and its count; the block never shows, and a press on the stroke opens the mark's own card. The export, which writes every mark whatever the switch, writes the stroke in the place's Hebrew (carrierWithStroke) and nothing at the mark's own position: the verse as the edition prints it.
