The card names the stem it held the rows to, and says the stem's count on the whole form of a divided word

Two faults on one line of the card, both reported by readers and both proven here before they were touched.

The stem was never named. Under "only this pointing" a row whose own source files it under another stem than the word has at this place is withheld and counted. The card said how many and never said than which (an outside reader, 12 November: "the stem is not named on the card", though the page holds it and withholds by it). I had marked it as owed with the next page change and shipped a page change without it earlier today.

    before   113 of 304 pointed otherwise, withheld · 5 of another stem, withheld
    now      113 of 304 pointed otherwise, withheld · 5 of another stem than qal, withheld

The names are the lattice sidecar's own, the ones the gate reads: qal on 38,378 pointed surfaces of the thirty-nine books, hiphil 8,532, piel 5,426, niphal 3,767 and thirty-three more. 292 surfaces carry two names, where the place's two sources disagree, and the card says both ("than hiphil or qal"). The sentence the card prints when its switches leave no record names the stem too. The gate is not changed: what is withheld and what stays is what it was, and the stem is said only where it withheld something.

The count was dropped on a copy. The card keeps a filtered copy of a pool in two places: the whole form of a word its place divides (the readings of the word in its parts are filed under the division), and the written half of a pair. Each copied the pool's counts by name, and the stem's count was not among the names (a reader of the page's code, 10 November, finding 7; on my list of faults reported and not yet proven). Proven on the live page:

- Deuteronomy 22:29, word 17, the whole-form division: 37 readings and no withheld line at all, where the stem had withheld 9 store rows. Now: "9 of another stem than piel, withheld".
- Amos 4:2, word 10, the whole-form division: "14 records withheld by the pointing ... No record is left to read here", with 7 more rows withheld by the stem and unsaid. Now the sentence carries both.

One function, carryWithheld, names every count a pool carries and both copies take them all, the stems with them. By a count over the store's rows (a place with two or more pieces taken as divided, so a close count and not the page's own): of 142,852 places where a divided word's whole form has rows in the store, the stem withholds a row at 457 (1,215 rows); at 91 of them readings are left and the withheld line now carries the count, and at the rest the sentence does.

tools/check-pointing-gate-v1.mjs holds both. Both pass on this page and both fail on the live one.
