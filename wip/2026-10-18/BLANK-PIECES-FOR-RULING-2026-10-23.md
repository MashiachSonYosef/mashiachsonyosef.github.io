# Blank pieces: the shapes, for the owner's ruling (23 October)

Moses's blank pieces v1 (FOR-ELIJAH-v82, candidate only): every piece at every
place whose division source gives it no English, read from piece store v2.
33,056 blank claims at 21,107 places, of 1,296,994 claims read. The page
draws nothing new for a blank until a ruling per shape; today a sign-only
piece stands as a gap with the signs switch off (the no-blanks pill prints
its sign), and a piece with no reading stands as a gap either way.

Two kinds of blank:

- SIGN_ONLY (29,930): the source wrote a sign alone, "<obj.>", "<to>",
  "[the]". TAHOT 20,738, MACULA 9,192. The no-blanks pill already answers
  these: on, the sign prints.
- NO_READING (3,126): the source wrote nothing, not even a sign. MACULA
  3,097, TAHOT 29. Nothing on the page can print these from the source.

Three rulings would settle nearly all of it:

1. The object marker את standing whole before a maqaf (14,034 claims, both
   sources blank at 13,934 of them): draw it as a gap, print its sign
   ("<obj.>", with the pill), or print nothing and let the maqaf chain's
   next word carry it. Today: a gap; with the pill, "<obj.>".
2. A prefix one source leaves as a sign where the other reads a word (ה
   "the" 4,944; ל "to" 1,312; ב; מ): keep the source's own sign (today), or
   let the line read the other source's word there, named as that source's.
   The second is a line that mixes sources at one word; the card would say
   so.
3. A NO_READING piece where the other source reads the same piece (פני
   "before" 464, כן "therefore" 311, אות "them" 344): the same choice as 2.
   Where neither source reads it (OTHER_BLANK_TOO, 13,957 claims, nearly all
   את), there is nothing to print; the ruling in 1 covers it.

Not a ruling, a note: the 29 TAHOT NO_READING claims and the 36 claims with no
letters are the small remainder; they stay gaps whatever is ruled.
