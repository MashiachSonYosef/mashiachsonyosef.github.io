# The words the two lanes use, in plain English (23 October)

The owner: "you two have lingo that isnt sensical to me". One line each.

- **zone, bin**: one work on the shelf, as one file (data/zones/<slug>.bin): its words, its line table and its receipts. The 39 Tanakh books are 39 bins.
- **the store, route store**: every dictionary's readings for every Hebrew key, one file per 256 shards; the card's pills come from it.
- **key, letters key**: a word's bare letters (no vowels, no marks). Everything is looked up by it.
- **reading**: one English wording a source gives for a key. A pill on the card is one reading.
- **record**: one dictionary entry, as received, byte for byte: the reading, the definition, the source, the year.
- **M, M id**: a source's number (M14 is Strong's, M5 is BDB, M20 was Bolls). D is the definition text. R is the reading.
- **the line**: the English printed under a word on the page. Baked into the bin as the line table, one reading per key.
- **the card, the hud**: what opens when a word is pressed: its readings as pills, the record beneath, the sources.
- **pill**: one pressable reading on the card. The pressed pill is what the line shows.
- **chip**: the small license label on a reading or a line.
- **piece**: a part of one word: a prefix (ב "in"), the core (שנת "year"), a suffix.
- **division, compspan**: one way of cutting one word into pieces (ב + שנת). A word may have several divisions.
- **block**: one piece of a division, as a pressable button on the card.
- **chain**: words joined by a maqaf (על־פי), read as one unit.
- **span**: a dictionary entry whose headword is two or more words (פת־בג "portion"), so the line can run under both words. The span store holds them.
- **run**: a named multi-word unit from the corpus lane's record (a run card draws one card for the whole run).
- **lattice, pg**: the corpus lane's per-place record: what TAHOT and MACULA read at each place, piece by piece.
- **TAHOT, MACULA**: the two per-place sources (Tyndale House's and Clear Bible's word-by-word English).
- **order**: which reading leads the line: oldest first, the place's own (TAHOT here), the characters, the corpus, the witnessed, a license class.
- **column**: a baked alternative line table for one order, holding only the keys where it differs.
- **witnessed**: an order the corpus lane derived from which sources agree at a place.
- **blob**: the gold outline drawn around a word's pieces on the page.
- **sign, sign-only piece**: a source's mark standing alone where it gives no English ("<obj.>", "<to>", "[the]"). A gap on the page is a piece with no reading or a sign the switch hides.
- **the door**: the front page. **the porch**: the plain-text readings under a chapter page for crawlers.
- **relay, FOR-ELIJAH**: a hand-off from Moses's lane: a store or ledger with its rule, receipt and counts.
- **receipt, pin**: a recorded sha256 of a file so a check can prove it has not moved. The shelf's pins are in zone-store-v1.json.
- **the gate**: the serve gate: which works the door may serve, judged file by file.
- **strike**: removing a source from the store whole, by a ruling (Bolls, 17 October).
- **check**: one script asserting one rule against the data and the page; 155 of them; the suite is all of them.
