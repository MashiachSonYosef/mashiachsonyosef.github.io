# The toggles, as the owner names them (23 October)

The owner: "i really need to be the one driving for a specific toggle so just kind of list them". Nothing here is built until the owner names it. One line each: what it does, what it needs, where it stands. "Needs Moses" means a store or ledger the page does not have; "ready" means the page has the data today.

| toggle | what it does | needs | stands |
|---|---|---|---|
| no blanks | a sign-only piece prints its sign instead of a gap | nothing | live, off by default |
| no names | a name-led line reads the entry's first reading | nothing | live, off by default (the "past the spelling" rule shown, not baked) |
| oldest first | the oldest dictionary's reading leads | nothing | live on the rail as the default order |
| newest first | the newest dictionary's reading leads | the rule: by the reading's year, newest; the card's order already knows the years | ready |
| source | one dictionary leads wherever it has a reading | nothing | live on the rail as the source switches (a source on or off); a "lead with this source" position is ready |
| makes sense | a sign-only or empty piece reads the other per-place source's word at that place, credited to it; else the sign | Moses's blank pieces v1 (here, candidate) | ready to bake |
| randomizer | a random admitted reading leads at each word, the same each visit on one device | nothing | ready |
| Christian preferred | readings from the Christian lexica lead (Strong's, BDB, TAHOT...) | a per-source tradition flag the owner rules | needs a ruling |
| modern Judaism preferred | readings from the Jewish lexica lead (Jastrow, Klein, Even-Shoshan where licensed...) | the same flag | needs a ruling |
| Masoretic preferred | the reading that agrees with the Masoretic vowels leads | the Masorah filter on the rail already has "only" and "letters" | partly live |
| most commonly selected | the reading readers press most leads | a count of presses the site does not keep (it keeps nothing of a reader) | needs a ruling on keeping counts |
| TAHOT at this place | the per-place witness leads | nothing | live as the "place" order |
| English reader | the cards with English on top | nothing | live |
| blobs, card numbering, concordance hud | as named | nothing | live |

The lingo the owner set: **span** for every multi-piece unit; a **mini span** is a word cut into pieces (prefix + stem); a **mega span** is a maqaf chain or two or more separate words read as one. The lanes' older words (compspan, megacompspan, chain, run) map onto these and are kept only in file and tool names.

## 24 October: no blanks parked; a refinement the owner named

The owner, 24 October: "to me no blank toggled on would probably include not allowing it to come back as <obj.>, maybe im wrong but rn my thinking is thats basically a blank", then: "dont overworry about the no blanks toggle if anything we could move it to /toggles/ for now if you have a lot of other stuff to serve up". Done the second way today: the no-blanks pill is off the row and the Menu, the switch stays on the rail and on /toggles/ beside no names. The refinement stands here for when it is built: with no blanks on, a line whose lead reading is a sign alone (<obj.>, <to>, [the]) is a blank too, and should read the first plain reading its entry carries under the order in force, with that reading's own license chip; the sign prints only where no plain reading exists. The engine's "signs after plain readings" position already orders the card's pills that way; the line would need a baked plain column beside the lead (as the no-names column was baked), and a measurement of how many lines lead with a sign alone across the 39 (not yet taken: the count was started and stopped to make room for the serving lane's relays).
