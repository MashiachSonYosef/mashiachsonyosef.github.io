How. The pool takes the word it is asked for (opts.word: its pointed surface, its own key, its verse); with none it is the word standing open, so a card's pool is the pool it was. The live line names each key's own surface, kept with its stand. The exporter asks first of the pool the word's own card stands on, then of the whole pool as before, so a reading the file found until now it still finds; a ruled line goes straight to the whole pool, as a ruling outranks the pointing as it outranks a switch.

A consequence, named. The file follows the card where the card withholds an older record as pointed otherwise, and a license class can move with it. At a word in pause (Amos 2:10's Egypt, with a qamats) the card withholds Strong's entry, filed under the ordinary form, and credits the concordance, whose row the filter keeps.
