     edge the numbers stand on. With the numbers on it is drawn over the same
     place by the row's own ::after, whole until seatNumbers writes the gaps
     (--rule-img), and the border or shadow that drew it keeps its room,
     unseen. A band of linen over the line would not do: the card's linen is
     a veil over the woven ground. (A pair, written and read, wears no line.) */
