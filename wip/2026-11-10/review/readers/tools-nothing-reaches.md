C. WHAT BELONGS IN THE ROUND

Four steps belong in the round's scripts, because every input they read is in the repository, they are deterministic, and forgetting them leaves the site inconsistent.

- bake-overlay-book-counts-v1 belongs in finish.sh, after the serve gate receipt is settled and before build-front-door-v1, which copies its numbers into every book page. It has already been forgotten once (the Tikkunei Zohar, and the overlay pins since 7 November). One law in a chain check should hold its books to the served set and its pins to the overlays' indexes.
- apply-ledger-divisions-v1 and then apply-source-years-v1 belong at the head of pipeline.sh, stopping the pipeline on a refusal. pipeline.sh's own first comment already states them as its precondition, and every store-changing hand step (a list, a ledger, a years list, a span re-bake) needs both before the regloss. Chain A recounts them (D1, D3, D8; Y2, Y3), but only after the long regloss. They do not belong in finish.sh: both rewrite timestamps on every run, which a page-only round should not carry.
- The restamp-zone-v1 loop belongs in finish.sh right after the projected zones are installed. It is a no-op when the stamps agree, and it closes the trap your README describes, where finish.sh installs the projected copies over zones restamped in place.

The rest is rightly run by hand, because its input is a corpus-lane delivery, a reviewed decision or another machine:
- strike-rows-v1 needs a reviewed list and a reason.
- The store bakes need a delivery: bake-dictionary-store-v1, bake-piece-store-v1 with its patch and blank ledger, bake-span-store-v2, bake-form-of-pills-v1, bake-read-form-offsets-v1.
- The seven projectors and verify-v-ledger-v2-v1 need a ledger delivery, and their outputs do not depend on the store.
- emit-masorah-witnesses-v1, emit-corpus-atlas-v1, emit-mam-restore-v5-rights-v1, run-restore-v5-fleet-v1, run-fleet-v2, apply-lone-letters-v1 and emit-work-attachment-v1 need the corpus lane's files or the whole shelf.
- restore-shelf-from-history-v1 is machine setup.
- build-wordmark-v1.py needs fontTools and an owner's pick; a small check holding its eight typed colors to zone.html's :root would replace the memory it now relies on.

What should be written down for these is the order of the followers, as in B2. Before anyone runs it again, project-run-cards-v1 should take its book list from the 39 rather than from the disk.
