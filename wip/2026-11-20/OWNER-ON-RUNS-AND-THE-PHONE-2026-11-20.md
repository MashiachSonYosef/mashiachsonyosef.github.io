## Runs, measured on the live tree (the 39 books)

- **The lines are already word by word.** Of 305,412 resting lines, 5 stand over more than one word (Daniel 1:13 and 1:15 «food», Jeremiah 39:3 «Nergal-sar-ezer» twice, Song of Songs 6:12 «Amminadib»). Bethel reads «Beth-» «-el», Ben-Hadad «Ben» «Hadad», in STEP's words.
- **The cards are not.** 40,980 runs joined by a maqaf hold 83,406 of the 305,431 words (27%), and each such word opens the run's card.
- **The twelve file a closed-up or as-written form for 869 of those runs (2.1%; 137 distinct), and most of it is accident:** two words closed up spell another word. אל־הים «to the sea» → «heavenly beings», אל־מלך «to [the] king» → «Allammelech», מה־לך → «journey», על־מה → «girl», כי־לי → «scoundrel», אם־לא → «I will complete». On the whole store (the dictionaries that rest shut included) 1,425 runs have such a form.
- **What the twelve really file over two words (the span store):** 850 of its 2,845 places carry a row of the twelve, under 253 distinct keys. 239 keys at 580 places are names (Bethel, Ben-Hadad, Beersheba, Esar-haddon, Bene-jaakan); the rest are a few fixed pairs (כי אם «except» 109 places, אם־לא 73, כי־אם 43, אף כי 19). By source: STEP's TBESH 433 rows, the lexical index 416, Strong's 315, UBS 310, the SDBH 288, BDB 171 and 118, UHAL 159, BiblicalTools 89.

So the owner's assumption is nearly right. The twelve read word by word but for two-word names and a few fixed pairs they file as one entry.

## What ship 10 builds from it

- At rest on the 39 books, **every word opens its own card**, an ordinary card with every gate (the pointing, the entry, the names). That removes the fault of ship 9's suite (a run's card asked its words with no gates) by removing the run's card, not by mending it.
- **The 850 places stay reachable:** where one of the twelve files the two words as one entry, the word's card offers that entry. Said to the owner; dropped too if they say so.
- Every other joined reading waits for a dictionary that files it, added on purpose.

## The phone

- **The three faults are reproduced, and all of them sit on a run's row.** As a Pixel 7's Chrome (412 wide, a phone's settings): Isaiah 2:9 וַיִּשְׁפַּל־אִישׁ reads «everyone + he has become low + anc», «and» cut and its blob past the card's edge (`shots/isaiah-2-9-…png`); 9:12 «it» cut; 12:2 כִּי־עָזִּי, the blobs' lines through the letters and their vowel marks (`shots/isaiah-12-2-…png`); 1:6 לֹא־זֹרוּ, the blob's corner at the «p» of «pressed out» (`shots/isaiah-1-6-…png`).
- **Counted over Isaiah:** 27 of 18,773 English rows are cut by their own row, every one a run's row and none a single word's, and the same 27 at 360 and at 412 wide. So it is not the phone's width: a single word's cell widens to its English, and a run's row does not.
- Why the probes of 19 November missed it: they asked whether text left its cell, and this text is cut inside its own row (a row that hides what runs past it).
- **The Hebrew face.** The page ships its English face as a web font and no Hebrew face: it asks for Frank Ruehl CLM, David Libre and SBL Hebrew, which a phone does not have, so each device draws the Hebrew in its own font (this machine in FreeSerif; an Android phone in a Noto Hebrew face). The faults above show in all three faces tried, so the face is not their cause. It is still why this machine and the owner's phone do not draw the same page: to bring to the owner, with candidates and their licenses checked.
