## A correction to ship 9's record (from v202)

Ship 9's record and its commit say four lines went from right to wrong at the places of אֵלָו, «to, towards, into» → «behold» (Ezekiel 9:4, 40:34, 40:36, 1 Samuel 22:13). **Two of the four were not right before:** STEP tags אֵלָו at Ezekiel 40:34 and 40:36 as H0352B, «pillars», and the lattice's lemma there is MACULA's אַיִל. So «to» was wrong there too, and those two went from one wrong reading to another. «To him» is right at three places: 1 Samuel 22:13, Ezekiel 9:4 and Zechariah 2:8. v202's form rows put the right word at all five: under אלו, «to/ him» (H413, H9033) and «pillars/ its» (H352, H9023), each place taking its own by the entry gate. So of ship 9's 32 moved lines: 22 wrong → right, 6 one reading for another (not 4), 4 right → wrong (not 6).
