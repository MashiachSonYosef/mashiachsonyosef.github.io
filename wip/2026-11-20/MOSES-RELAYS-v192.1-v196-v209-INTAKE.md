## A correction to ship 9's record (from v202)

So «to» was wrong there too, and those two went from one wrong reading to another. «To him» is right at three places: 1 Samuel 22:13, Ezekiel 9:4 and Zechariah 2:8. v202's form rows put the right word at all five: under אלו, «to/ him» (H413, H9033) and «pillars/ its» (H352, H9023), each place taking its own by the entry gate. So of ship 9's 32 moved lines: 22 wrong → right, 6 one reading for another (not 4), 4 right → wrong (not 6).
