# The store under 10 GB: what it holds today and what the cut takes (20 November 2026 AD)

Page lane (Elijah), read-only on the store. The owner, 20 November: «i guess the main goal for this week should just be getting the R2 to be free then which i think means under 10gb?»

## The free allowance

Cloudflare's R2 pricing page: free each month are 10 GB-month of Standard storage, 1 million Class A operations (writes and listings) and 10 million Class B operations (reads); egress is free; deletes are free. Past that, storage is $0.015 per GB-month. Storage is billed on the month's average, so a month counts as free only if the store stays under 10 GB through it. (https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/, read by search on 20 November; the page itself is not reachable from this machine.)

## What under 10 GB takes

1. **The old layout goes (163 GB).** Moses runs the deletions (the owner, 7 November). Before it: everything the live build pins must stand in v2. On 8 November 158 pinned names were not in v2 (compspan v13.8's span ledgers, the identity bridges, the C0 maps, the first store's route inputs, the shelf's 21 unserved zones). `v2/corpus/compspan-v13.8/` is there now; the rest is to be checked again. The page lane's shelf backup (`serving-lane/`, 0.64 GB) is the last whole copy of 3,441 unserved works as built (letter of 10 November): it moves into v2 or the owner lets it go.
2. **v2's text is stored compressed (45.5 GB → about 7 GB)** and each file once (the 9.65 GB of repeats).
3. **That leaves about 28 GB, so v2 is pruned to what is current:** logs and search outputs (the 1.54 GB stderr log, source-census's catalogue candidates 3.3 GB), superseded runs inside a ledger (compspan-backwards's runs 5 and 6), a verify seat's staged copies. Moses knows which.
4. **The sources are the hard part: 12.69 GB, already compressed, and the one thing nothing else can rebuild.** With them the store cannot be under 10 GB. The scans (8.92 GB) came from the Internet Archive; the store could keep each scan's identifier and hash and let the bytes go, or the scans live on a disk of the owner's. This is the owner's call.

Without step 4 the store ends near 15 to 20 GB, about 10 to 15 cents a month. With it, under 10 GB and free from the first whole month it stays there.

The owner on the plan: «sounds like a good plan id like this to be permanent even if i do no work». The letter to Moses carries it: `FOR-MOSES-2026-11-20-the-store-free-for-good.md`. What changed in this map after a closer read of the listing (tools and the recheck in `r2/`):

Old layout only: the shelf's 21 unserved zones (`serving-lane/shelf/`, which stays) and one C0 map in `corpus-staging/`. So from the page lane nothing else holds the old layout back.
- **«The scans» are not scans.** `v2/sources/ia-scans` is the Internet Archive's own text files (`.djvu.txt.gz`, 3.83 GB in 16,175 files), Moses's token tables built from them (`.tokens.csv.gz`, 4.63 GB, one per raw file) and records (0.46 GB). The tables can be made again; the raw text can be fetched again by identifier.
- **Kept once and compressed, v2 is about 25 GB:** ledgers 7.2, corpus 7.2, sources 10.3, letters 0.04. Large and not current by their names: `v2/corpus/lattice-ledger/candidate` 2.92 GB (the served lattice is v12, 0.33 GB) and `v2/corpus/shelf-lattice/designs` 1.44 GB.
- **A ceiling of 8 GB is proposed**, with shares: sources nothing can fetch again 2.5, the Archive folder's records 0.1, corpus 2.5, ledgers 2.0, the shelf backup 0.64, letters 0.1. With the Archive's raw text kept the total is 11.7 GB, so it leaves too, with each file's identifier, name, size and sha256 kept.
- **What keeps it free with nobody watching.** R2 has no spending cap (budget alerts only send an email), usage is rounded up to the next GB, and storage is the average of each day's peak. So: only a lane at work writes; every ship states the total and refuses over the ceiling; a new version replaces the old in the same ship; text goes up compressed; scratch lives under one prefix that a lifecycle rule expires; Standard storage only; no public address on the bucket.
- 847,518 objects carry a November date, so this month's writes are near the free million; deletes are free.
- The site itself never reads the store: everything served is in the repository.
