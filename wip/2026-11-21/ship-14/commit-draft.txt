Ship 14: twenty-one faults readers reported, proven on the live page and mended; @@REST@@

Five readers went through the page's script on 10 November 2026 and reported faults I had not yet proven (the review, section 1). Each was decided again in a browser on the live build (c1ed776b65) before anything was changed, with one more from a later note (a card that moves when a second pill is pressed): of the 24, 3 were gone and 21 still stood in whole or in part. This ship mends the 21, and what was left of one of the three. The record: wip/2026-11-21/ship-14/SHIP-14.md, with every report, probe and patch.

A PAIR WRITTEN ONE WAY AND READ ANOTHER

- A ruling on the written half of a one-word pair was remembered under one key and looked up under another. Any repaint gave the line back to the read half (Daniel 2:43 word 1), and a ruling on a plain word spelled like the written half took the pair's line over (Daniel 2:8). The ruled half is now asked under the key it was remembered by; the same where the read branch has several words (Deuteronomy 33:2 word 15).
- A ruled written form lost its chip at the first repaint. It keeps it.
- A pair's line and chip ignored the source switches: in Ruth, with STEP's translation switched off, 6 of the 12 pairs still printed its words under its chip. A half whose reading has every carrier switched off now reads its baked alternate, or nothing, and its chip names whose reading that is.

THE CARD

- A card moved when a second pill was pressed on a phone: at Obadiah 1:1 word 1 its top went from 172.7 to 92.7 and 124.6px of page stood between the card and its word. The clamp now holds a placed card at its word, and the reset pill's line is written without moving the open card's word. Cards also open at their word where live left some away (Obadiah 1:1 word 3: 28px).
- A still press on the card's head marked the card as dragged, and the next word's card opened where the old one stood. The card is parked only after six pixels of travel.
- A reading chosen through its witness line was remembered under a key no reading answers to, and the card opened again on another pill (Ruth 1:2 word 3).
- A switch pressed over a block with no reading drew the block before it into the band.
- In the concordance, a dictionary shard that failed was asked again from inside its own failure: about 1,500 requests in five seconds from one press. One failure is one request, asked again when the reader presses the block again.

THE LOAD AND THE EXPORT

- A network fault at load said «No book is published on this page yet». A fault now says it is a fault, with its reason; a book the shelf does not hold says what it said.
- Four loaders (the lattice, the source-suggested file, the map of pointers, the toggle ledger) kept one failed fetch as an absence for the whole visit. A fetch that failed is let go and asked again when next needed.
- Every book page asked for two run files before it drew and laid neither at rest (Genesis: 22,708 and 53,278 bytes). They are asked for the first time a run is to be laid.
- The resting-off default of a source was stamped as given though its file had not arrived, so it never rested off for that reader again. The stamp waits.
- An exported verse was gated by the pointing of whichever card was opened last: a ruled reading was listed as withheld (Amos 4:10 word 3 after the card at 3:7 word 6). The export asks for no word.

THE PANEL OF SWITCHES AND THE SEARCH

- The panel's one line counted ledger ids: «13 dictionaries · 1 off» on a fresh load with every chip on. It counts what the fold counts, the chips a reader sees off. This changes that line at rest on every book.
- The box on a «whole source» line did nothing. It switches its source off and on as the chip does, and the panel being read stays open.
- The search asked the dictionary for the letters as typed, so a word typed without its final letter showed another key's readings or none. It asks for the word it found.
- After a switch drew the panel again the scribes' marks button was gone from the page (behind the whole panel's address).

A SECTION LAID AGAIN, AND A RUN'S LINE

- Laying a section again left an empty bar row behind and lost an opened commentary (behind the commentary's address); the pairs' ledger and the ledger of where each form stands were never pruned.
- A reading pressed on a block of several words inside a run was not read by the run's line (Genesis 35:7 words 6 to 8: the card «El + Beth-el», the line «El + Beth- + -el»).
- With joined words as written: only the first head word of a run's own card was bold; a run line left with one reading kept the smaller size its row was let down to; a run line gone bare kept its title.
