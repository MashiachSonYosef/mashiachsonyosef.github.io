## What still stands that the reader called not right: 59 pills

Every one is a piece cut from a longer entry of its dictionary (the store's row holds the whole entry beside the piece). 58 are Strong's, 1 is BDB's.

- 39 «another stem»: single renderings from Strong's list of how the King James renders the root, in every stem. «select», «feed», «choose», «dispatch», «do» under «create» (Genesis 1:1). Strong names no stem, so nothing of his sets them apart.
- 9 «bundled»: a piece that still holds several renderings, «(be, cause to be, make) fruitful».
- 7 «another word», 3 «unsure», 1 «broken» («set with sow(-er)»).

Uncut, each entry would be one long pill, which the reader called «bundled» and not right. The cut does not make Strong's words wrong; it sets each rendering alone, without the stem or the sense it belongs to.
