THE PAGE

- The words of a unit take the unit's one line, read at the place it is filed. Standing in one cell (a stroke joins them, or a run holds them) the line prints once over them. Standing apart, each keeps the line as it rests and the card says what the source reads across them. This is what the page already did at the units it could tell; the unit is now told by the source's data first.
- A line filed beside the words it reads (UBS's «Aram Zoba» at the object marker of II Samuel 10:6) is not printed under the word it is filed at.
- A unit that holds a pair, or a word that yields to a switch, is not read as one: none of its words takes the source's line.
- The card of a word of a unit gives the unit's reading as «one reading of these N words», and the source's other picks at that very word after it.
- Found on the way, on the site before this ship: a joined run's card said a unit's reading at every cell, «Bethel · sense 1 + Bethel · sense 1» at Amos 3:14:10 under BDB, over a line that read it once. The card says a unit once.
