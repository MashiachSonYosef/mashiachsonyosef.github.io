THE STORE READER
  openRouteStore(dir, { atRest: true }): routesFor gives the rows that rest open ([] where every one rests
  shut); routesWhole gives them whole; atRest = { rule, record, version, sha256 } (null opened whole);
  restsOff = row -> the resting entry or null (null opened whole); glossFor's reason
  EVERY_READING_RESTS_SHUT; tableFor's counts.every_reading_rests_shut. It reads the record from the
  store's parent folder (reader/data) and throws one sentence when the record is missing or does not hold.
  The projector refuses a book in scope whose zone carries no stamp, or a stamp of another sha256, and a
  zone out of scope that carries one.
