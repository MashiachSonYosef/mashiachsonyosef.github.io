WHAT THE READ-OFF TOOLS NOW SAY OF A PAGE
  state.kinds   "shut" | "open" where the page says the dictionaries that rest shut act on the book
                (window.__kindsHere), null where they do not (the Tikkunei Zohar, a commentary, a fixture).
                Same expression in resting-lines-v1 linesInPage, card-readings-v1 cardsInPage, gated-card-v1 cardInPage,
                so a chain book's state at rest equals its book's resting-lines record (check-chain-book-v2 K3).
  lifted        card-readings STATES.lifted.keep adds "fh.kinds": "open@<record version>" (read from
                data/dictionary-kinds-v1.json at import), holds adds kinds: "open"; readCards refuses a lifted
                reading whose page reports otherwise.
  refusal       linesOnPage (and so readAtRest, readCards, openGatedCard) throws where window.__kindsFailed:
                a page that was to act and could not take the record draws span lines over shut rows.
  gated card    openGatedCard(..., { kinds: "rest" | "open" }); "open" loads ?kinds=open; anything else throws.
