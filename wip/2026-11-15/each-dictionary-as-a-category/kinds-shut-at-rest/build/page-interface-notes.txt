Working note of the page builder, 15 November 2026. Plain facts, for parts 4 and 5.

WHAT THE PAGE READS FROM A ZONE
  zone.emitted_from.rests_off present  -> the kinds act on this page (addition 1). Absent -> nothing new is drawn.
  .entries[<entry id>].ids   {M: n}   whole-source entry: the rail's chips, one per source key with n > 0
  .entries[<entry id>].rows  number   the rail's line "N records on this book <say>"
  .version / .sha256                  read only for checks; the card follows the record as fetched
  (keys, keys_bare_at_rest, scope... are not read.) Part 4's in-progress regloss writes exactly this shape.

THE RECORD
  fetched beside the store's index: data/dictionary-kinds-v1.json. restsOffOf/restsOffIds run over a copy of
  the store's own index.m_sources taken before the overlays join it. A record missing or not holding:
  window.__kindsFailed = true, window.__kindsFault = the sentence, nothing gated, the rail block says so.

HOOKS FOR CHECKS
  window.__kinds        "shut" | "open": the reader's choice (also on the Tikkunei Zohar, where nothing acts)
  window.__kindsHere    true only where the zone is stamped and the record held: the gate acts
  window.__restsOffIds  the 27 M ids shut whole (restsOffIds)
  window.__kindsVersion the record's version
  localStorage fh.kinds "open@<record version>" | "shut"; ?kinds=open|shut for one load (not kept)
  card: .r-pills[data-kind-withheld] (pills held back), button.kinds-switch[aria-pressed] on the withheld
        line and on an emptied card, pill button.rests[data-rests="1"] = stands on shut rows alone
  rail: #kindsRow (inside the sources strip's fold, above "shelve by"), .kinds-opt[data-kinds=shut|open],
        .kind-chip[data-key][data-ids][data-family] (disabled while shut; never .dfp)
  railNow's sources token: its title says "N more rest shut"; its text does not count them

WHERE THE GATE STANDS IN poolFor
  after the entry, names, pointing, piece, stem and source switches gates, for the store's rows (routes), and
  after the overlays' pointing and stem for extra: the span store's rows carry their source's own M (Wikidata
  M2 «the Jewish people» at Exodus 1:1) and rest shut with it. Overlays' own ids answer null.
  storeOnly pools (the live line) are always gated where the kinds act; everySource (exporter) and
  everyKind (the masthead's name) lift it.
