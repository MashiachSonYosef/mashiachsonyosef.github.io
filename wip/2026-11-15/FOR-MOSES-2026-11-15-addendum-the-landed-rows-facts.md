## What the page found

v188 lands 4,201 rows (ship 4). None of them carries facts (slot 10): no entry numbers, no name mark, nothing the row entries of v190 give the other rows. Counted on the ship's store, row by row from the row-strikes record: M3 1,801 · M5 219 · M23 210 · M17 175 · M60 167 · M19 163 · M4 152 · M21 122 · M13 115 · M18 106 · M6 103 · M9 80 · and the rest under 100 each.

So neither of the page's gates can judge them. The entry gate reads a row's entry numbers; the names gate reads its source's name mark. A landed row stands on every card whose key holds it, whatever entry the place reads.

- **On the Bible pages** the line is the witnessed order's, so 20 resting lines move. On the cards the landed rows stand as pills no gate can judge.
- **On the Tikkunei Zohar** the line is the store's reading carried by the oldest source, so a landed row that joins a reading makes it lead. 3,626 lines move, most for the better: «debt» to «Moses» at 137 places, «Ijob» to «Job» 84, «Jirmejah» to «Jeremiah» 71, «hitherto» to «now» 133; 754 words that stood bare now carry a line (568 «this»). One class is worse: Strong's rows that are a person's or a place's name now lead at a word that is not one. «young» to «Bani» at 96 places, «fat» to «Beri» 65, «know» to «Jada» 52, «them» to «Ham» 24, «number» to «Minni» 24, «do» to «Esau» 21, «dust» to «Epher» 20, «mandrake» to «Dodai» 19, «north» to «Zephon» 19.

The page will not judge these rows by their English. The rows are Strong's own, landed by your list.

## Ask 9. The facts of the 4,201 landed rows

Send them in the shape of v190's row entries (by key, rank, source and text, read at the store after v188), each row with what its source says of it: its entry numbers, and for the sources whose name mark the names gate reads, whether the source marks it a name. With those, the entry gate and the names gate judge the landed rows as they judge every other, on every page.

## A note for the Tikkunei Zohar's line

The Tikkunei Zohar has no witnessed order and no word marked a name, so the oldest carrier decides its line. If your lattice can carry an order for it, or a mark on the words that are names, its line would stand on what a source says of the place, as the Bible books' does.
