# Other pages and their builders: every color use, for the metals

Survey of 15 November 2026 for the palette change (the owner's three metals). Part: **other-pages**, every served page outside `reader/zone.html` and the tool that writes it. Read-only: the live site's source at `scratchpad/old` (git, main `ace7f07eba`), served at http://127.0.0.1:8914. Nothing under `old/` or `pt/` was written. Owner's words read from `wip/2026-11-15/the-palette/THE-OWNERS-WORDS-2026-11-15.md` as it stood at 01:55 UTC (50 lines, items 1 to 19 and «Where the palette stands»).

## How this was surveyed

- **Pages listed off the tree, builders found by the paths they write.** `find` lists 1,293 HTML files outside `reader/` (1,292 `index.html` and `404.html`); every one is generated (section 1). The one hand-written page is `reader/zone.html`, which is the other part's.
- **Served bytes held to their builders.** The style block of `index.html`, `census/index.html`, `demonstrations/index.html` and `palette/index.html` is byte for byte `DOOR_CSS + STAMP_CSS` as the builder holds it today; the style of all 13 chain pages is byte for byte `CHAIN_CSS`; the style of `toggles/index.html` is byte for byte the toggle builder's; `reader/engine-b23f95d61a8c.css` is byte for byte zone.html's largest `<style>` (its sha256 begins `b23f95d61a8c`). So every row below names the builder line that makes the served line, and the served line is given beside it.
- **Every color-bearing declaration parsed, not grepped** (`work/css-colors.mjs`: comments blanked, nesting kept, a declaration counted when its property carries color and its value holds a hex, rgb(), var() or a named color). Colors inside `data:` URLs (the linen weave) and inline `style=` attributes were added by hand.
- **What each rule paints was asked of the served pages** (`work/probe-door.mjs`, `probe-door2.mjs`, `probe-census2.mjs`, `probe-pages.mjs`): every selector, with :hover, :focus and pseudo-elements stripped, counted on the door, the census, the demonstrations and the palette at load; then on the door after a word's card was opened and a reading pill pressed, and after typing «ruth» in the search; then on the census after a book's card was opened. No screenshot was taken; one existing picture was looked at (`toggles/shots/row-signs.png`).
- **Hebrew on these pages was read for its color** (`work/probe-hebrew.mjs`): door 68 Hebrew texts, census 2,933, all `rgb(165, 32, 53)` (#a52035); the demonstrations and palette pages print none; **no text on the four pages carries a Masoretic sign** (0 with U+0591–U+05C7). So gold, under the scheme, appears on these pages only in the woven name and in the palette's swatch.
- Work files: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-mashiachsonyosef-github-io/c72302ec-11e7-5b6d-a5ab-47bb077585fe/scratchpad/v57/other-pages/work/` (the decoded stylesheet `door-css-full.css`, the parsed rows `door-colors.tsv`, the probes' counts `door-state.tsv`, `chain-probe.tsv`, `toggle-probe.tsv`, and the generators of this file).
