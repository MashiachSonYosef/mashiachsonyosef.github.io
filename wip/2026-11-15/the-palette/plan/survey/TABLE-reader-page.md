Surveyed 15 November 2026 on the live site's source at `old/` (main ace7f07eba), read only. ## What this part covers, and who loads it

- `reader/zone.html` (17,179 lines) carries the reader's whole stylesheet inline (lines 21–2379), its markup (2380–2553) and its engine script (2554–17177). The page at `/reader/zone.html?b=…` loads no other CSS.
- `reader/engine-b23f95d61a8c.css` (2,358 lines) is byte for byte the body of that `<style>` (both hash to `b23f95d61a8c`); engine line = zone line − 20. `tools/build-front-door-v1.mjs:2754–2788` extracts it (and `engine-948ef173fc14.js`, the script, engine line = zone line − 2553) and writes the `<link>` into the built pages. **1,272 pages load it**: every book page and chapter page of the 39 books (e.g. `/obadiah/1/index.html:30`), `demonstrations/` (8) and `tikkunei-zohar/` (296). The door (`index.html`), `404.html`, `palette/`, `toggles/`, `census/`, `chain/` do not.
- The markup (2380–2553) paints no color: no `style=`, `fill=` or `stroke=` attribute (searched). The script sets or reads color in the places listed at the end of §3.
- **Print: none.** No `@media print` and no `matchMedia` anywhere in zone.html or the engine CSS; the only export is a Markdown file (`zone.html:5978`, `text/markdown`), which carries no color.
- **Counted**: 678 color references in 522 rules (each var(), hex, rgb/rgba, transparent, currentColor, or color: inherit, one row each), plus 35 token definitions in :root and 15 script rows. 59 declarations on color properties name no color (`none`) and are left out. Of the 678, 188 reference the gold family (--gold, --gold-dim, --line, --line-c, --chip-line, --mark-ink, --sel-text). No hsl(), color-mix() or named color other than transparent/currentColor is used; the only hex values are the 10 in :root.
- Confidence: **sure** = the scheme names it or S2 painted it so; **likely** = follows the scheme by the page's own rule, not shown to the owner; **owner's call** = not clear under the scheme: a question below, with a recommendation.
