## What the page lane does with them

- **Nothing agreed is held.** What the owner and the lane agreed is built and shipped on the lane's own proofs (the round, the suite, the gate, the live check). The owner meets it on the site while reading and says what is wrong there. The site is where things are tested.
- **Where the lane has a recommendation, it takes it.** A design choice inside what was agreed (a shelf shut or open, a label's wording, an order) is the lane's to make and to ship; the owner corrects it on sight. A choice is put to the owner only where the lane truly cannot move: something not agreed, that cannot be undone by the next ship, or that the owner's own rules pull two ways.
- **A question that must be asked is one line, first in the message, alone.** Never inside a write-up, never a list of them, never a re-asking of what was agreed. Work that does not depend on the answer goes on.
- **Length is not the fault; burying is.** (Corrected by the owner's fourth word, below: the lane first read word 3 as a call for short messages.) A message may be as long as it needs and may sound like the lane. What it must not do is hide a question, an expectation or a hold inside itself, arrive a day after the work with the work still waiting on it, or ask again what was agreed. The long record (measures, tables, reasons) still lives in these notes.
- **An example names a class.** A card, a word or a chapter the owner or the outside reader names is read as an instance. The answer and the fix are at the level that mends every case: a dictionary, or a kind of entry within a dictionary. A card is never edited; a row-by-row list is the wrong shape.
- **No letters awaiting an answer.** The files of questions for the owner (OWNER-QUESTIONS-..., "ship 7: after the answers") stop being gates: each open item is settled by the lane's recommendation and shipped, or, where it truly needs the owner, asked as above.
- **The models.** Work already running finishes on the allotment it started under; what is launched after the owner's fourth word runs on the model the owner switched to. If a helper is refused for a limit, the lane says so at once with the refusal's own words, and does not assume a limit it has not just met (the owner's correction of the morning: «not true bro please do it now»).
- **Removing is not a taboo.** (Word 5. The lane had hardened the rule of 14 November, «the card makes no claim», into «nothing leaves unless its own source says so».)
