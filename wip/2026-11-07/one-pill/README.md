Found by check-proper-name N5 on the label-off build: at עמוס Strong's «Amos.» (M9) and Kaikki's newly printed «Amos» (M13, B05 label_off) stood as two pills that read alike, and the name's carriers in gloss_m named M9 alone, so with Strong's off the name pill stayed. The rule now: a reading's identity in the pool is its printed form, case folded (readingKey: the "/" pieces joined with " + ", one closing stop dropped), in tools/gloss-store-v1.mjs (rule v6, exported), the page's pool and its reading matches, the projector's carriers test, the credit tool (gloss-m-v1) and the checks that re-derive carriers (check-carrier-survives, check-read-form-offsets). Why a lead moves: the joined reading takes the best rank any witness gives it, so a Strong's reading at 1890 that a modern row ranks first now leads among the 1890 readings (היה «happen» → «calamity», Strong's «calamity.» joined with TBESH's «calamity» at rank 2; אבל «but» → «Abel»). On a Bible word the line is the place reading, so this reaches the card's first pill and the line of the words with no place reading; in the commentary zones and the Tikkunei Zohar, whose lines are the oldest-first lead, it moves 3 to 5 percent of keys (Isaiah's commentary 186 of 4,440; the Tikkunei Zohar 296 of 8,242).
