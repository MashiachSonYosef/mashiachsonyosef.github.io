# The entry measure, 16 November 2026 AD (plan of 17 November, section 1, day 1)

Live tree afea14aac6, at rest under «only this pointing», the store's rows (the dictionaries beside the store and the span store not counted). Tool and output beside this file: `measure-entries.mjs`, `measure-entries.json`. The live gates are applied in order: entry, names, pointing (v5 and the unpointed rule).

## What it found

- **The place's own entry is almost always known.** STEP's tag gives an entry at 298,947 of the 299,808 judged places (99.7%).
- **Most readings at rest already carry an entry.** 8,935,079 of the 9,688,331 row-and-place pairs standing carry an entry (row facts `n`): 92.2%. Every Bible dictionary carries one on 97.5% to 100% of its standing rows: MACULA, STEP, Strong's concordance, the unabridged BDB, BDB, UBS, Strong's, SDBH, the Living Lexicon and STEP's brief lexicon.
- **The rest is the entry ledger's whole scope in the store.** 41 sources carry no entry, 725,620 standing pairs (7.5%):
  - the Kaikki strict source M25: 268,773;
  - English Wiktionary M13: 263,318;
  - the Classical Hebrew Words database M23: 85,497;
  - Hebrew Wiktionary's form tables (M41, M96, M98, M74, M36): about 65,000;
  - Kaikki's forms M11: 20,970;
  - English Wiktionary's Aramaic M60: 12,495;
  - Kaikki's Aramaic M72: 7,224;
  - and the smaller tables.

  The span store, Jastrow and the Samaritan dictionaries come on top of these.
- **Places that stand on their own entry alone.** At 136,845 of the judged places (45.6%), every standing reading is of the place's own entry.
- **Card size is not mainly an entry problem.** Distinct readings standing per card: median 13, 90th percentile 87, 99th 190, max 305. The long cards are the place's own entry many times over: Strong's concordance lists every King James rendering, and MACULA and STEP list their renderings at every place the letters stand.

## What it means for the plan

- **The entry ledger fixes the wrong readings**, and its scope is now known: the Wiktionary family and the Classical Hebrew Words database, then the span store and the dictionaries beside the store. Sent to Moses (addendum to `FOR-MOSES-2026-11-16-the-plan-for-next-week.md`).
- **The card at rest fixes the card's size** (plan section 2). The next measure is what a small card would hold: per source, the place's own pick or the entry's first reading, with the rest a press away. Count it per card and set it beside the median 13 and the 90th percentile 87.

## The second measure: how small a card at rest could be (plan section 2)

Same tree and gates (`measure-sources.mjs`). Distinct sources standing on a card at rest:
- every source: median 3, 90th percentile 10, max 15;
- the Bible dictionaries alone (MACULA, STEP, Strong's concordance, the unabridged BDB, BDB, UBS, Strong's, SDBH, the Living Lexicon, STEP's brief lexicon, the Open Scriptures index): median 3, 90th percentile 8, max 10.

So a card at rest showing **one reading per source** would hold about 3 readings (10 at the 90th percentile), against today's 13 (87 at the 90th percentile). Each source shows its own pick at this place where it has one, and otherwise its entry's first reading. Every other reading of the same entry stays one press away under its source.

## The card for the owner (plan section 2, to put)

> **What a card shows at rest.** Today the median card shows 13 readings, and one in ten shows 87 or more; the long ones are the same entry many times over (Strong's concordance lists every King James rendering; MACULA and STEP list their renderings everywhere the letters stand). *Recommended:* one reading per source at rest, each source's own pick at this place, or its entry's first reading. That is about 3 readings on a typical card and 10 at most for nine cards in ten. Each source's other readings stay one press away under its name. Nothing is removed; the card gets short.

## The third measure: an entry where STEP gives no tag (plan section 1, step 3)

`hp-entry.mjs` (live tree). STEP gives no tag at 861 judged places, and MACULA's lemma (the zone's `hp`) stands at 757 of them. Looking the lemma up under its own key in the store gives the entries of the rows whose headword is the lemma exactly: 754 places get one or more entries. 349 get exactly one; the rest get several, homographs such as «tent» and the name Ohel (H168, H169). At 654 of them, the word's own rows include that entry, so the entry gate would keep them whatever their points; 1 Samuel 2:10's «about» (H5922, under the lemma עַל) is of that kind. Proposed for ship 8: the place's entry falls back to MACULA's lemma where STEP gives no tag, in the page's gate, the projector and the checks that model it (`tools/entry-gate-v1.mjs`).
