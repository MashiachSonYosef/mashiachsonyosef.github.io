# A chapter opened as a phone reader would, 16 November 2026 AD (plan, big picture 2)

Live tree afea14aac6, served locally. Tools beside this file. The phone is Playwright's Pixel 5 profile. The local server does not compress; GitHub Pages compresses text files (JSON, JS, CSS) but not the `.bin` files, which are gzip already. So the over-the-wire figures are estimates (`gzip -6` of each text file).

## Where the bytes go (Genesis 1; raw, then estimated over the wire)

- **Before the words:**
  - the lattice sidecar `zones/genesis.lattice.bin`: 1,579 KB raw, 1,579 KB over the wire;
  - the zone `zones/genesis.bin`: 1,073 KB, 1,073 KB;
  - the engine `engine-*.js`: 1,137 KB, 357 KB;

  In all, about 3.2 MB over the wire before a word shows: about 16 s at 1.6 Mbit/s, about 3 s on a fair 4G link.
- **For the first card:**
  - the whole book's piece store `piece-store/genesis.json`: 8,595 KB raw, 2,007 KB over the wire;
  - `default-picks/genesis.bin`: 258 KB, 258 KB;
  - `source-suggested/genesis.json`: 762 KB, 85 KB;
  - `form-of-pills-v1.json`: 630 KB, 107 KB.

  In all, about 2.4 MB more: about 12 s at 1.6 Mbit/s.

## The two biggest wins (page lane; no reading changes)

1. **Fetch the lattice sidecar after the words paint**, or with the first card if nothing at rest needs it before: 1.6 MB, about 8 s at 1.6 Mbit/s, off the time to the words. First confirm what at rest reads it (the line's leaders are baked into the zone).
2. **Split the piece store by chapter** (or serve it per verse): the first card on a chapter of Genesis would fetch about 40 KB instead of 2 MB. The bake is `tools/bake-piece-store-v1.mjs`; the page's `pieceBookReady` asks for the book's file.

Then measure again on the same three pages. Over the wire is what counts; a local server with gzip would make the measure honest (to do).

## Win 1, its cause (read in `reader/zone.html` at afea14aac6)

- At load (about line 5150), a setting that reads the lattice fetches the sidecar at once and repaints the lines when it lands: a lattice order, the Masorah switch other than «keep», or names as sound. The default is «only», so every page asks for the 1.6 MB sidecar beside the zone, before its words show.
- The lines at rest are baked into the zone under the gate (`lineBakedWithoutGate`, about line 3137), so the repaint at rest should move nothing.
- **Proposal:** start that fetch once the first section is drawn (idle), not at load. Keep the immediate fetch for a reader whose remembered setting is a lattice order, «the letters only», or names as sound. Prove it with the resting-lines recording (no line may move) and the Masorah checks, then measure the three pages again.
