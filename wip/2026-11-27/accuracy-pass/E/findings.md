# Helper E: the sources off by default, held or refused. Which could come back, and which were we applying badly

Measured on the frozen tree (main ea6e1ad08e), 27 November 2026. Nothing was changed, nothing shipped.

## The answer in short

- **None can reopen as it is.** The best of them is right for the word 72% of the time as served today.
- **We were applying most of them badly, in one way above all.** On a word that carries a prefix or a suffix (145,023 of 305,431 words) the card opens on the word's core piece, and there the page asks a row neither its pointing nor its stem. A row with no entry number is then bound to the place by nothing. This is the cause of the owner's own example of 19 November (Isaiah 1:22, Jastrow's «sproutings» under «dross»).
- **Two can reopen after a mend the page lane can make from data it holds:** English Wiktionary's Hebrew entries (only its rows whose entry is the one STEP's tag names: 86% right in the sample) and Wikidata's lexemes (once the piece is asked its pointing: 89%).
- **Four need the corpus lane first.** One of them is the best thing we hold back: STEP's own Expanded Strong tags, which give STEP's sense and name identity at each verse and word. Landed by letters they would be a disaster; served at their own place they are STEP serving as STEP serves.
- **The rest should stay off.** Where they are right they repeat the twelve; what they add is modern Hebrew, rabbinic usage, names and apparatus.

## How this was measured

- **By machine, every word.** `walk2.mjs` rebuilds, for each word of the 39 books, the block its card opens on (the one core piece of STEP's division, else the word whole: zone.html line 10933). It puts every row under that block's key through the page's gates in the page's order, with the kinds switch read as open: entry, name, pointing with the lattice's fallback, piece class, stem, the source's own switch, Jastrow's word senses, the signs switch. Counts are in `walk2-counts.json` and `families.tsv`.
- **Checked against the page.** 840 sampled placings of 14 families were opened in a browser with `?kinds=open`. 830 stood on the card as the model said (98.8%). Of the 10 that did not:
  - 7 were two gaps of the first model that `walk2.mjs` closes: 6 at words whose only vowel is qamats qatan, which the page grades by the lattice, and 1 hidden by the signs switch;
  - 3 were cards that open on a run of words, which the model does not rebuild (0.4%).
  - Of the 139 placings drawn afterwards (the redrawn Samaritan sample, the replacements, the two own-entry samples, the Aramaic-places sample), 138 were found on the page; the one other was set aside for its sign, and the page indeed does not offer it.
- **By hand.** 60 placings per served family across 17 to 26 books, drawn uniformly over (word, pill) and never the same (letters, reading) twice. 60 per held list (30 for Harkavy), read from the corpus lane's list against the card's block, because nothing of them is served. One reader made every call. A sample of 60 carries about ±12 points.

## Findings, by how many places they touch

### 1. A core piece is asked neither its pointing nor its stem (145,023 words; verified on the page)

- **What happens.** A card opens on the word's core piece wherever STEP divides the word (core-block-rule-v1, zone.html 10933). A surface that is not the open word's own key is asked with `pointed: null` (zone.html 3562-3566). The pointing grade needs a pointed surface (line 8953) and so does the stem gate (line 9043). So on a piece only the entry gate and the names gate act. The comment at line 8871 says so: «The gate grades only the open form today».
- **Count.** 3,039,636 row placings of the served off-by-default sources stand on a core piece with no entry of the place. Graded against the piece's own pointing (cut from the word's surface):
  - 513,389 point as the piece does;
  - 1,195,737 are pointed otherwise where another row attests the piece's pointing;
  - 1,120,956 carry no pointing there.
  - So 2,316,693 (76%) would be withheld.
- **On the page.**
  - Isaiah 1:22 word 3 (the lamed and «dross»): Jastrow's «growth», «sproutings», «luxuriant growth» stand. Their headword is pointed with a doubled gimel; the piece is not. Graded, they are «pointed otherwise», and four store rows attest the piece. The record of 19 November put this down to the dagesh; the grade does weigh that dagesh. The cause is the piece.
  - Ruth 1:1 word 4 (the + judges): Jastrow's «judgments», «punishments» and Hebrew Wiktionary's «punishment» stand. Deuteronomy 16:18 word 1, the same word with no article: none of them; the card says «1 pointed otherwise, 1 not pointed by its source».
  - 1 Kings 1:1 word 1 (and + the + king): 31 off-by-default pills, Jastrow's «to advise» and «what is it to thee?» among them. Proverbs 30:27 word 1 (a king, bare): 8.
- **In the sample.** Jastrow is right at 11 of 17 whole words and 8 of 43 pieces; the ETCBC 11 of 13 and 10 of 41; Wikidata 16 of 18 and 22 of 42.
- **Class.** Not a rule of ours overriding a source. The gates that reconcile several words under one spelling are missing at one kind of block, so the source's own answer (its pointing, its stem) goes unread.
- **Mend (page lane, data held).** Give the core piece its own pointed letters, cut from the word's surface by the piece's span. Grade it as a whole word is graded, with `beginsWord` false (the flag is already in `gradeRow`). Ask the stem the same way.
- **Effect with the sources opened.** Jastrow 1,277,387 pills to 611,750; PanLex 829,106 to 293,074; English Wiktionary's Hebrew entries 1,203,097 to 1,027,805.
- **Suspected, not measured here.** The same gap stands at rest for the twelve's few rows that carry no entry.

### 2. The unpointed lists stand only where no gate asks (186,184 word placings of four served lists)

- ConceptNet stands at 75,096 words, 74,563 of them a piece; MUSE 30,775 of 31,197; Hebrew Wikipedia's titles 35,524 of 35,646; Lexibank 44,138 of 44,245. Davidson's held lines would at 60,097 of 60,622.
- On a whole word the owner's ruling of 16 November holds them back («if its no pointing why would that in your head auto qualify it for all pointings rather than none»). On a piece it is not asked. Whether such a row shows depends on whether the word happens to carry a prefix.
- **Class.** A ruling of the owner's applied at one kind of block and not the other.
- **Mend.** Finding 1's. After it the four stand at 6,976, 3,405, 2,337 and 2,528 words: nowhere that matters, which is the honest state of a source nothing can bind.

### 3. English Wiktionary's Hebrew entries: the entry list is what makes it right

- 53,581 of its 123,748 rows carry Strong's numbers from the corpus lane's entry lists (relays v203, v204, v211, v218). At tagged places its row placings are 560,544 of the place's own entry, 418,895 of another entry (withheld today) and 428,755 with none.
- **Sample, own-entry rows:** 38 of 44 right word, 0 another word. The 6 wrong are modern senses: «brit milah» under covenant (2 Samuel 3:12), «cabinet minister» under official (1 Kings 22:26), «verse (part of a song)» under household (Psalms 68:7).
- **Sample, no-entry rows:** 5 of 16; 7 another word: «To export» under he brought out (Job 12:22), «to restore» under month (2 Chronicles 15:10), «-less» under to me (Jeremiah 1:9).
- **What its own record gives and we do not read** (the dictionary store serves the record; `survey-kaikki.mjs`):
  - a part of speech on all 25,663 records;
  - the verb's construction on 11,003 senses (pa'al 4,023, pi'el 3,096, hif'il 1,481 and on);
  - «Modern Israeli Hebrew» 320, colloquial 343, slang 301, misspelling 1,045, «Biblical Hebrew» 761.
- **Mend.** Page lane: stand only the rows whose entry is the place's. Then, from the record we already serve: rest shut the senses it tags modern, colloquial, slang or misspelling, as its names already rest by its own mark; and hold its verb construction against the place's stem.

### 4. Aramaic entries stand on Hebrew verses (50,499 of 52,915 pills)

- The source writes «lang: Aramaic» on every record. Only 2,416 of its pills fall in the Bible's Aramaic stretches.
- On Hebrew verses: «to shave» under the letter (Jeremiah 29:1), «needle» under who makes a sin offering (Leviticus 6:19), «Sabbath» under to dwell (Deuteronomy 13:13).
- **Class.** The source's own answer (its language) unread.
- **Mend.** Serve it only at Aramaic places. The page lane can do it from a table of the stretches (Daniel 2:4 to 7:28, Ezra 4:8 to 6:18 and 7:12-26, Jeremiah 10:11); a per-word language from the corpus lane is cleaner. The same holds for Jastrow's Aramaic entries and Cowley's glossary.

### 6. Hebrew Wiktionary: one in three own-entry readings is a modern sense

### 7. The held Bible-dictionaries list (18,164 lines)

- **STEP's Expanded Strong (1,431 lines).** Each line is one of STEP's sub-senses or name identities and names the verse and word it was read at.
  - By letters: «penis» under his hand at Isaiah 10:32 (STEP gives it at Isaiah 57:8 word 17); «copulate» under to a burnt offering at Numbers 7:27 (STEP: Genesis 31:10); «Jerusalem» under Yahweh at Isaiah 41:17 (STEP: Isaiah 60:14).
  - In the sample 25 of 60 are the word's other sub-senses and 12 are other places' names.
  - Where the sub-sense happens to be the verse's own it is exactly right («age» under from a son of twenty years, Ezra 3:8).
  - This is the principle's own case: serve STEP as STEP serves.
- **The ETCBC (4,246 lines).** Right at whole words (11 of 13), not at pieces (10 of 41). Its verbs are unpointed roots. 341 lines are descriptions in angle brackets («<uncertain>» 280 times by relay v215), which the signs switch hides (14,557 pills).
- **Davidson's other editions (12,487 lines).** The book prints every inflected form pointed, with its parsing and root. The lines hold bare letters (0 pointed) and fragments of a rewording. By the lane's own relay v217: 6,436 are readings the book prints, 3,387 stand under its name marks, 1,489 are not in the entry and 962 could not be found. They would stand almost only on pieces.

### 8. Smaller ones

- **The translation tables print the English headword alone.** The source ties the Hebrew to one sense of an English entry; the sense stays in the record. «old» under daughter of (Genesis 26:34; the sense is «having lived for the specified time»); «of» under a lamed in Daniel 3:22 (the sense is telling the time); «lev», the Bulgarian coin, under its heart (Job 41:16).
- **The Samaritan works print glossary apparatus as readings.** «pronounced שמה, and so rhymed: in alphabetical hymns = ש.» under to Yahweh (Judges 5:3); 28 of 60.
- **The signs switch hides marks that are our build's, not the source's.** English Wiktionary's Samaritan lemmas carry wiki brackets («[[cosmos]]»), and Cowley's rows carry the lane's «[Arabic]» placeholder. The switch reads both as a source's sign and hides 15,654 pills.
- **Uhlemann's Latin stays off** under the owner's ruling of 3 October even with the kinds opened: 205,215 placings withheld by its own switch, and 0 of his pills on 60 probed cards.

## What I checked and found clean

- The kinds record files every M id of the store and both overlays: 0 unfiled rows of 653,353 and 118,793.
- The entry gate does its work on the sources that carry entries: 418,895 other-entry placings of English Wiktionary's Hebrew entries withheld, and 0 of 44 sampled own-entry pills another word.
- The names gate: 25,163 placings of English Wiktionary's name rows withheld at places that are not names; Davidson's name marks would withhold 36,195.
- Jastrow's word senses are held back as ruled (79,914 placings).
- The page folds a «defective spelling of» or «plural form of» row into the reading it points to. Among 60 sampled Wiktionary pills only one bare usage note stood.
- The model against the page: 830 of 840, and 138 of the 139 drawn afterwards.

## What I could not check, and why

- **STEP's Expanded Strong and the ETCBC at their own places.** I hold their lines, not the per-place tables. The right rate there is by construction for STEP and unmeasured for the ETCBC.
- **Why English Wikipedia's openings hold no served row.** The 18 M ids are in the index, no shard carries a row of them, and the row-strikes record names no round for them.
- **Cards that open on a run of words** (3 of 840 probed): not rebuilt by the model.
- **Prefix and suffix blocks**, one press past the card as it opens: reach counted (`words reached (any block)` in `families.tsv`), not sampled.
- **«What it adds»** is a lexical test plus one reader's calls. It cannot tell a new sense from a rewording, and I say so where it matters.
- **The owner's «28».** I count 17 served families, 5 held lists and the language-refused groups. I did not find the list the figure 40 came from.
- **PanLex's held rows.** I read send back v1's file (3,956 lines); v1.1 withdraws 12 of them.
