# Helper A: the departures list, and the homonyms (the largest pass)

**Question.** On every one of the 305,417 words of the 39 books: where does what our page offers depart from what STEP gives, and for each rule of ours behind a departure, how often did it settle a real conflict between sources, and how often did it act with nothing to decide?

**What STEP gives at a word:** TAHOT's division of the word into pieces, each piece's tag (Strong's number, with a letter where STEP extends it), and each piece's gloss. Find where the zone carries these (`pg` pieces on words, the lattice sidecar, `tools/entry-gate-v1.mjs` placeTags / placeEntry) and, for ground truth, TAHOT as received on the store (`v2/sources/`, or the path the STEP family's record in `data/dictionary-kinds-v1.json` and `data/source-corpus-v1.json` name).

**Part 1, the rules inventory.** List every rule of ours that withholds, reorders, joins, cuts or adds something on a card or a line at rest. Start from: the entry gate; the pointing grade (and inside it the joining dagesh and the grading of one-letter pieces as prefix or suffix); the names gate; the stems (stem families); «unpointed rows: none by default»; held places; a pointer never leads; the span grade; runs and joined cells; the default picks' order; and the maqaf pair's options (`coversOfRun` in zone.html: the owner's own example, «we added the maqaf COULD cut as a weld, but then had 0 welded valid definitions, and still added it to every maqaf card»). Find any others by reading zone.html's card code. For each rule: its name, where it lives (file:line), what it reads from a source, and whose ruling stands behind it (the code's comments quote the owner with dates).

**Part 2, each rule counted two ways, over all words, for the twelve Bible dictionaries' rows.** For each rule, count (a) the placings where it changed what a reader sees, (b) of those, the placings where two sources really disagree there (the division, the entry, or the pointing differs between the row's own source and STEP), and (c) the placings where nothing needed deciding: the row's own source agrees with STEP's entry for that piece and the rule still withheld it; or the rule offered something no source gives (a maqaf pair no dictionary files as one entry). State your operational definition of (b) and (c) for each rule; where a rule cannot be split this way, say why. Give five example places for each cell, chosen from different books.

**Part 3, homonyms.** Every placing where a row of one of the twelve stands on a card by default (not withheld by any gate) while the row's own entry (its Strong's number, STEP letter or BDB entry, as `factsOf` gives them) is not the one STEP's tag names for that piece. Count by dictionary and by cause (the row carries no entry so no gate could judge it; the gate was not asked; the gate judged it and let it through; other). Separately: rows withheld by the entry gate although their entry is STEP's (the gate's false alarms).

**Part 4, the line.** 124 words have a line at rest that is not STEP's (`data/resting-lines/`: credit not `PIECE:TAHOT` and not `M4`). List all 124 with place, the Hebrew key, the line, its credit, and what STEP gives there (a gloss, an empty gloss, nothing). The lead is reading these too; your list is the machine side.

Use `S/s29w/tools/name-flips-20.mjs` as the pattern for walking every place and asking each gate. Check your gate model against the real page on at least 40 cards chosen across books (`probe-cards-20.mjs`), and report how many agree: a model that disagrees with the page is the first thing to mend.

Output: `findings.md`, `rules.tsv` (one row per rule), `departures.tsv.gz` (one row per placing counted in (c)), `homonyms.tsv.gz`, `line-not-step.tsv`.
