# Auditor Q: is every line at its word? An audit that does not lean on the builders

Launched 27 November by the lead. `S` = the session's scratchpad.

**What it audits:** a frozen copy of the fresh build as it stood after the trial page's third cut, `S/s47w/frozen/place-answers-v1/` (the page files are what the card prints).

**Against what:** the raw source files only, `S/s45w/fetched/`, read by its own small readers. It may read the builders' reports and the page's code to learn a page file's shape; it may not import, run or copy the builders' libraries to read a source or to decide what belongs at a word.

**The sample:** every 1,000th word of the 39 books (about 305), and up to 40 of each hard class found from the page files' own marks: written and read (both forms); written and not read; read and not written; spelled otherwise than STEP's; one word for two rows and two for one; verses numbered otherwise; a UBS line reached through MACULA's dictionary form; a number that stands on several records of the unabridged BDB; the words with no reading from STEP or from MACULA.

**The checks, per word, from the raw files alone:**

- a. STEP: the row at the reference the page prints holds the parts and numbers the page prints; its Hebrew is our word's, or differs exactly as the page says; its place among the verse's rows agrees with our word's place.
- b. MACULA: the table's rows for the word hold the English the page prints; the Hebrew is this word's.
- c. UBS, «cites this very word»: the sense lists this verse and word; and the word it means is this one (the entry's lemma is MACULA's form here, or its Strong's code is STEP's number here).
- d. BDB, «cites this verse»: the record or sense has a reference to this verse; and the record is one STEP's number reaches by the files' own numbers.
- e. «In the dictionaries»: each one line is from the entry the raw file holds under the number.
- f. Sixty cards read as a person reads: does every line belong to this word?

A line that cannot be confirmed from the raw file is «unconfirmed», never «passed». The report is its final message; the lead saves it here as `Q/hand-back.md`.
