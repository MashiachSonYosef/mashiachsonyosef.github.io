**Subject: Five small slips in the English column of TAHOT**

Hello,

Thank you for TAHOT. We read it word by word and credit it on every word we show.

At revision 5de0a47f35f0fc13dfe1430d104e5db8a4285f9c, the English column has five places that look like slips: a stray mark, an unbalanced bracket and one misspelling. Each is given below with its row id, the Hebrew, and the English exactly as the file has it.

1. **Zep.2.9#26=L** וְ/יֶ֥תֶר, «and/ [the] rest ofK: ». The «K:» looks like a ketiv mark that has slipped into the English. The verse's own translation line has it too.
2. **Ezk.36.23#05=L** הַֽ/מְחֻלָּל֙, «that/ has been] profaned». There is a closing bracket with no opening one.
3. **Isa.7.23#15=L** וְ/לַ/שַּׁ֖יִת, «and/ <into> the>/ bush[es]». There is a stray «>» after «the». The verse's word 12 has «<into> the/ thorn[s]» with no stray mark.
4. **Lev.11.40#01=L** וְ/הָֽ/אֹכֵל֙, «and/ [who/ [-ever] eats». Two brackets are opened and only one is closed.
5. **Psa.123.4#08=Q(K)** לִ/גְאֵי/ /יוֹנִים, «of/ arrogant/ /opressors». «opressors» for «oppressors».

We show each row as you write it and have changed nothing. We only wanted you to know.

With thanks,
