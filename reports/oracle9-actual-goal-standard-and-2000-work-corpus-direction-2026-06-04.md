## License Lanes

- `commercial_clean`: Public Domain, CC0, CC BY, and other Agent-6-approved commercial-clean sources.
- `noncommercial_educational`: CC BY-NC only after Agent 1/6 custody, attribution, storage/display, and noncommercial boundary; `commercial_export_allowed=false`.
- `conditional`: CC BY-SA or other share-alike/complex licenses only if Agent 6/legal pipeline explicitly marks compatible for the target package.
- `metadata_or_link_only`: external-link-only, metadata-only, no-derivatives, unknown, proprietary, or uncleared sources.

NC does not contaminate the whole corpus. It marks derived rows for exclusion from any later commercial export.
