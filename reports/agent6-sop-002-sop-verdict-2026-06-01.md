## Rationale

SOP-002 satisfies the required corrections from the Agent 6 protocol verdict:

- It replaces unsafe `Agent 5 as QA worker` language with Agent 5 as `control/queue coordinator under Agent 6 QA authority and Agent 7 mission strategy`.
- It states Agent 5 is not QA authority and may not issue QA conclusions or acceptance.
- It defines Agent 7 law publication as mechanical publication of Agent 6's exact signed boundary.
- It separates protocol clean pass, protocol warn-accepted, protocol blocked, SOP clean pass, SOP warn-accepted, and SOP blocked.
- It requires docket path, verdict, effective boundary, warning limits, blocked uses, and unaccepted scope before law publication.
- It preserves `blocked_no_render`.

The SOP is signed with WARN rather than clean PASS because the title includes `QA Execution`. That phrase is allowed only under the interpretation that Agent 6 executes QA verdict/disposition by docket. It must not be used to imply Agent 5 performs QA acceptance or that drafting checks are QA conclusions.
